## FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 2019-09-16 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 6,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $1,106.01, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares are fully vested upon issuance.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

5. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

6. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

7. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

8. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.
