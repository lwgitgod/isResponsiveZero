# FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of July 11, 2020 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 5,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $6,706.04, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares are fully vested upon issuance.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

5. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
