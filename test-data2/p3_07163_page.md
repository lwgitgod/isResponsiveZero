# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 2023-11-23

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "management services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $24,263.40, retroactive to June 6, 2022;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Summit Trust without further Board approval.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The committee noted renewal of the general liability policy for information only. Management discussed the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office.

The Board deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations for information only. The Treasurer reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. Management deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar without further action.

The committee reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary requested an update on the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board.

The Treasurer reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. The Chair reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers. Management deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter.
