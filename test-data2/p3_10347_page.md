# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective 2025-02-09

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 23 days per year, up to a cap of 40 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar at least annually. New hires are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Contractors are encouraged to read the holiday schedule within thirty days.

New hires are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Team leads may request changes to the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Managers should review the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable.

Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. New hires may request changes to the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request.

Employees should review the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Contractors should review the safety checklist during onboarding. Managers are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.
