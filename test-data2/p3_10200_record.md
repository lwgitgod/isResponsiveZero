## Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2020-07-24

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 21 days per year, up to a cap of 33 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Managers may request changes to the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

Remote staff may request changes to the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Remote staff must complete training on the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Team leads are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Managers are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

New hires should review the safety checklist during onboarding. New hires may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Team leads should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Remote staff must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Contractors may request changes to the safety checklist at least annually. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.
