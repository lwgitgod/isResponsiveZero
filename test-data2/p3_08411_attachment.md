## RESTRICTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of June 19, 2019 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $6,637.38, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.

The singular includes the plural and vice versa, as the context requires. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void. The recipient may suspend performance in the event of delays caused by third-party vendors within ten (10) business days. Performance shall be rendered in a professional and workmanlike manner. The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference.

No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. The service provider shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request. The disclosing party shall provide reasonable documentation of the matters described in this section upon written request. The service provider shall provide reasonable documentation of any inaccuracy in the schedules to the extent permitted by applicable law.
