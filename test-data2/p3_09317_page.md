# TECHNOLOGY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective the 5th day of July, 2021, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline codebase, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 5,500,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request. The singular includes the plural and vice versa, as the context requires. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder.

Any successor shall provide reasonable documentation of any material change in circumstances in accordance with the notice provisions. Each party shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. The disclosing party shall indemnify the other party against any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions. The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of any inaccuracy in the schedules to the extent permitted by applicable law. The undersigned shall maintain accurate records of any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions.

The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees. The undersigned shall not be liable for any material change in circumstances during the term and for one year thereafter. Each party shall not be liable for losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. The undersigned shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations as soon as reasonably practicable. Any successor shall promptly notify the other party of delays caused by third-party vendors as soon as reasonably practicable.
