# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 12/9/2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $36,324.37 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake

### General Terms

Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail. Either party shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules during the term and for one year thereafter. The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding delays caused by third-party vendors as soon as reasonably practicable. Any successor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any material change in circumstances upon written request. The undersigned shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances upon written request.
