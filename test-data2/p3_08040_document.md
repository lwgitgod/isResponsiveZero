## CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated December 27, 2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $87,548.83 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

Either party shall promptly notify the other party of any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions. The service provider shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority upon written request. The service provider shall provide reasonable documentation of any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable.
