# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being all of the directors then in office of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective 2023-11-01:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 15,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $155.11.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-3 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "non-vesting".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Secretary deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary with no objection. The Treasurer deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Secretary deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines with no objection. The committee requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance.
