# Remote Work Policy
Effective 2020-02-03

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 24 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Managers must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. New hires should review the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Managers may request changes to the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Team leads should review the holiday schedule through the internal portal. New hires are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Team leads are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Employees are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. New hires must complete training on the holiday schedule within thirty days. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins.

Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Employees are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Contractors should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Remote staff should review the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Team leads must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. New hires are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule during onboarding. Remote staff must complete training on the safety checklist as soon as practicable.

Contractors are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Employees are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. New hires are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Managers are encouraged to read the holiday schedule within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Contractors must complete training on the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. New hires should review the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle.
