# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 6/25/2019  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Mei Schultz, counsel.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $52,854.20.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at First Coastal Bank.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

### Other Business

The committee deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Chair noted the status of state tax registrations for information only. The Board deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar and requested written materials in advance. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers.

Management noted the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. The Board noted the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action. The committee reviewed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Treasurer reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. The Secretary reviewed the status of state tax registrations with no objection.

The Chair discussed the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The committee reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Board noted the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance.

The Board requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Treasurer noted the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Chair discussed the status of state tax registrations for information only. The committee requested an update on the status of state tax registrations with no objection.
