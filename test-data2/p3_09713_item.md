# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2025-10-10

Purpose. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 19 days per year, up to a cap of 39 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Team leads should review the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Managers are required to acknowledge the safety checklist within thirty days. Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Managers should review the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.

Team leads are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.
