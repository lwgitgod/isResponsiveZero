CONFIDENTIAL

# WRITTEN CONSENT OF STOCKHOLDERS IN LIEU OF MEETING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Effective October 28, 2024, the undersigned, representing a majority of the outstanding voting shares, hereby:

RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed as a director of the Corporation, effective immediately;
RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed from the office of President, and Jordan Blake is appointed President and Chief Executive Officer;
RESOLVED, that the size of the Board is fixed at one (1).



The Board deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The Treasurer deferred consideration of the draft budget template with no objection. The Chair reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting and requested written materials in advance.

The Secretary noted the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Board discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action.
