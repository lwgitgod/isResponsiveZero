# COMMON STOCK SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of April 3, 2019 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $1,635.61, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares vest in equal monthly installments over 48 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

5. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.
