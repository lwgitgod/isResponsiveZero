# Remote Work Policy
Effective March 13, 2024

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 31 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Unused balances are handled according to state law. Remote staff are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Contractors may request changes to the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Employees should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. New hires may request changes to the safety checklist during onboarding. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Managers are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards at least annually.

Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Contractors are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff should review the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable.

Employees should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Managers may request changes to the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Managers may request changes to the updated leave calendar at least annually. Contractors are encouraged to read the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Employees should contact People Ops about the safety checklist at least annually.

Contractors may request changes to the updated leave calendar at least annually. Contractors are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule during onboarding. Employees should review the safety checklist within thirty days. Employees must complete training on the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable.

Remote staff may request changes to the safety checklist within thirty days. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff must complete training on the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Remote staff may request changes to the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Employees may request changes to the home-office stipend rules at least annually.
