# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: December 20, 2020  Place: offices of Orchard Row Counsel LLP
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Mei Adeyemi, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $76,702.58.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Harborview Savings.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

The Chair reviewed the annual compliance calendar for information only. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Chair discussed the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. The Treasurer requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance.

The Chair acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy with no objection. The Treasurer requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary reviewed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board noted the status of state tax registrations for information only.

Management requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary discussed the draft budget template with no objection. The committee requested an update on the draft budget template without further action. The committee deferred consideration of the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action.
