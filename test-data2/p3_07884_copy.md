Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 20 days per year, up to a cap of 31 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

### Procedures

Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Managers are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

Team leads may request changes to the holiday schedule within thirty days. Managers are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar at least annually. Contractors are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.

Team leads must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually. Contractors should review the holiday schedule during onboarding. Managers may request changes to the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Employees are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle.

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Team leads may request changes to the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Team leads should review the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Contractors should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure through the internal portal.
