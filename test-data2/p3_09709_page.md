# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted April 8, 2024

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "administrative services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $8,724.80, retroactive to 2022-10-18;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Harborview Savings without further Board approval.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

### Housekeeping

The committee discussed the status of state tax registrations for information only. The Chair deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. The Chair reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake.
