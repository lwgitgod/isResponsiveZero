## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

Effective the 22nd day of August, 2019, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline codebase, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 5,500,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

Any successor shall maintain accurate records of losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days.

The Company shall indemnify the other party against any claim arising hereunder upon written request. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any material change in circumstances in accordance with the notice provisions.

The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference. The Company shall indemnify the other party against any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter. The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter.
