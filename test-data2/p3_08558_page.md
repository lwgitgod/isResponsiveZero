# CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated April 28, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $85,513.10 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

6. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

7. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. The service provider shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules except as otherwise provided herein. Each party shall promptly notify the other party of the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions. The service provider shall not be liable for losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days.

The service provider shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned shall provide reasonable documentation of delays caused by third-party vendors except as otherwise provided herein. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of the matters described in this section during the term and for one year thereafter.

Each party shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations except as otherwise provided herein. Either party shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations as soon as reasonably practicable. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law.
