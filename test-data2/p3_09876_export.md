# LEGAL NOTICE — If you had an account with a regional telecom between 2018 and 2024, you may be eligible for a payment.

A settlement has been reached in Ortega v. SkyLine Wireless. The settlement fund is $18 million.

Claims deadline: 2024-03-07. Final approval hearing: 11/4/2020.

### Additional Provisions

Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. The Company shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section as soon as reasonably practicable.

Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. Any successor shall maintain accurate records of losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days.

The parties waive trial by jury to the fullest extent permitted by law. The service provider shall promptly notify the other party of losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. The recipient shall not be liable for any material change in circumstances during the term and for one year thereafter. The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days.
