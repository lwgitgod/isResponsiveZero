## RESTRICTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 2019-05-16 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,000,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $4,145.74, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares vest in equal monthly installments over 48 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

5. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.
