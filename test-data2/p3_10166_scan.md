## Lemon Garlic Pasta
Serves 6. Prep 16 min.

Ingredients:
- salt & pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp cumin
- 3 cloves garlic
- salt & pepper
- 1 onion

Steps: Preheat, combine, cook 55 minutes. Top with cilantro.
