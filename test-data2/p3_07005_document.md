# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 7/17/2024

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 21 days per year, up to a cap of 33 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.
