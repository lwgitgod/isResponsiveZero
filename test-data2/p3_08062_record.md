# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective 2024-10-07

Purpose. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 23 days per year, up to a cap of 40 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Team leads should review the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle.

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal.

New hires are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Contractors should review the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Contractors are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Team leads must complete training on the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Contractors should review the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
