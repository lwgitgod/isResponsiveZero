**PAID TIME OFF POLICY**
Effective the 18th day of May, 2021

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 13 days per year, up to a cap of 28 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Managers are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule within thirty days. Managers should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Team leads should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Contractors may request changes to the benefits enrollment window at least annually.

Employees may request changes to the safety checklist during onboarding. Team leads are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Employees should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Team leads should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Contractors are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Employees must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually. Team leads are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal.

Contractors should review the travel booking procedure at least annually. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Employees should review the updated leave calendar during onboarding.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Remote staff must complete training on the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Employees should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
