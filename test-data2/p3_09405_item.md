# Parental Leave Policy
Effective the 15th day of March, 2020

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 21 days per year, up to a cap of 31 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Team leads should review the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Managers may request changes to the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Employees may request changes to the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Managers are required to acknowledge the safety checklist within thirty days. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Managers should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards at least annually.
