# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being the sole director of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective October 14, 2024:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 25,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,650.94.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-4 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "failure to pay consideration".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The Treasurer deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. Management discussed renewal of the general liability policy without further action. The Chair discussed renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting.

The Treasurer requested an update on the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. The Chair noted the draft budget template for information only.
