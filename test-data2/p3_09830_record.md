# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 2020-05-04  Place: Denver, CO
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Rosa Novak, counsel.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $55,608.31.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 2,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

### Housekeeping

The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. Management deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar and requested written materials in advance. The committee acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting.

Management noted renewal of the general liability policy with no objection. The Board discussed the proposed meeting calendar for information only. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. Management discussed the status of state tax registrations for information only.

The Board noted renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. The Chair deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance.

The Board noted the proposed meeting calendar without further action. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. The Treasurer reviewed the draft budget template without further action.

The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. The Treasurer noted the proposed meeting calendar and requested written materials in advance. The Chair noted the annual compliance calendar without further action.
