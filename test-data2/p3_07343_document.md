# Remote Work Policy
Effective 2023-10-28

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 27 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

### Frequently Asked Questions

New hires are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Managers are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding.

Managers are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Contractors are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure within thirty days. New hires should review the benefits enrollment window at least annually.

Employees are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Employees are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Contractors should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Remote staff are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
