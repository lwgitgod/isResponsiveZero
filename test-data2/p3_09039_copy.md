# Independent Appraisal of Common Stock Fair Market Value
Subject company: Brightline Ventures, Inc.   Valuation date: the 22nd day of March, 2020
Prepared by: Ledgerpoint Appraisal Group

1. Engagement. The addressable market for mid-market workflow automation is estimated at several billion dollars annually and continues to grow as teams consolidate tooling.
2. Methodology. We considered the income, market and asset approaches and relied primarily on the backsolve method.
3. Discount for lack of marketability: 20%.
4. Conclusion. The fair market value of one share of Common Stock is $0.04.

Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.

Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.

The product roadmap prioritizes integrations with common accounting and CRM platforms, followed by a mobile companion app.
