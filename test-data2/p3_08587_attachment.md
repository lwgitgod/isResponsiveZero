## Brightline launches Brightline Mobile

Denver, CO — 2/19/2022 — Brightline today announced a public beta of its workflow automation product, adding QuickBooks sync.

"Setup now takes minutes, not weeks." said Tomás Chen, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on December 9, 2022. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Ivan Park of Harbor Retail.

Customer retention remained strong through the period, with most accounts expanding seats at renewal.
