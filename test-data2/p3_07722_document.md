# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted June 26, 2024

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "operational consulting services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $10,866.19, retroactive to the 8th day of July, 2021;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Summit Trust without further Board approval.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake. 
/s/ Jordan Blake
