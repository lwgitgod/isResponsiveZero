## Remote Work Policy
Effective the 9th day of April, 2024

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 26 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Contractors are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Remote staff must complete training on the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Team leads are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar within thirty days.

Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Remote staff should review the holiday schedule within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Managers may request changes to the safety checklist during onboarding.

New hires must complete training on the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Team leads may request changes to the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Employees should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually.

Managers are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Contractors should review the safety checklist through the internal portal. Remote staff may request changes to the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Unused balances are handled according to state law. Team leads may request changes to the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Contractors are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Contractors may request changes to the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Contractors are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers should review the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. New hires should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Contractors should review the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule at least annually.

Remote staff should review the safety checklist during onboarding. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the safety checklist during onboarding. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Employees must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days.
