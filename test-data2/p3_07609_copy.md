# CONTRIBUTION AND ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective the 9th day of November, 2021, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline platform, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

### Standard Terms and Conditions

The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. Performance shall be rendered in a professional and workmanlike manner. No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument.
