# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made the 15th day of May, 2023 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 678 Lake Dr, Madison, WI.

1. Services. Provider shall provide strategic advisory services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $7,791.44, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Three years.

4. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

5. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.
