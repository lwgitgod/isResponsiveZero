# CONTRIBUTION AND ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective the 28th day of April, 2021, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline scheduling engine, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 5,500,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.
