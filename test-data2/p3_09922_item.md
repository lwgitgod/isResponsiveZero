# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being the sole director of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective the 10th day of February, 2023:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 25,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,969.09.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-4 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "breach of founder obligations".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.



The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. Management requested an update on the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The Board acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. The Board reviewed the annual compliance calendar for information only.

Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Chair discussed the annual compliance calendar for information only.

Management discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting for information only. The committee discussed the proposed meeting calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board acknowledged receipt of the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved.

The Chair requested an update on the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. The Chair deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Secretary noted the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection.

The Board requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar and requested written materials in advance. The Secretary requested an update on the status of state tax registrations without further action. Management deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar with no objection.
