# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

Effective the 5th day of March, 2021, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline scheduling engine, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

### General Terms

The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. Either party shall maintain accurate records of the matters described in this section upon written request.

The service provider shall maintain accurate records of any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years. The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances during the term and for one year thereafter. Either party shall indemnify the other party against any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail.
