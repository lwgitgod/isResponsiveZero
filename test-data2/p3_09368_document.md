# Parental Leave Policy
Effective January 16, 2024

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 16 days per year, up to a cap of 22 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Employees are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Contractors should review the safety checklist within thirty days. New hires are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule during onboarding.

Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually. Contractors must complete training on the travel booking procedure through the internal portal.

Contractors should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Team leads should review the holiday schedule within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Managers may request changes to the updated leave calendar within thirty days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
