# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective May 22, 2024

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 21 days per year, up to a cap of 26 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

### Procedures

Remote staff should review the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Remote staff must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Contractors may request changes to the holiday schedule within thirty days. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Managers are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Team leads may request changes to the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule within thirty days. Contractors are required to acknowledge the safety checklist during onboarding.

Team leads should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Managers may request changes to the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. New hires must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Employees should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Remote staff are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal.

New hires should review the updated leave calendar during onboarding. New hires are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Remote staff must complete training on the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable.

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Managers must complete training on the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Employees should review the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Contractors are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule within thirty days. Contractors are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure during onboarding.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. New hires should contact People Ops about the safety checklist through the internal portal. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. New hires should review the home-office stipend rules at least annually.
