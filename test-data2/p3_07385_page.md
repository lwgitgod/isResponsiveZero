# Remote Work Policy
Effective the 8th day of September, 2025

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 27 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Employees may request changes to the safety checklist through the internal portal. Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Employees are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. New hires should review the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

New hires may request changes to the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Employees must complete training on the safety checklist within thirty days. Remote staff should review the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Managers should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Remote staff must complete training on the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Managers should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule during onboarding.
