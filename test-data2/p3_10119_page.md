# WRITTEN CONSENT OF STOCKHOLDERS IN LIEU OF MEETING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Effective the 8th day of April, 2024, the undersigned, representing all of the outstanding voting shares, hereby:

RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed as a director of the Corporation, effective immediately;
RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed from the office of President, and Jordan Blake is appointed President and Chief Executive Officer;
RESOLVED, that the size of the Board is fixed at one (1).

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

### Additional Matters

The Board noted the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The Treasurer discussed the draft budget template with no objection. Management deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy for information only. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board.

The Chair discussed the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. Management discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action. The committee noted the annual compliance calendar without further action. The Board requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar without further action. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved.

Stockholder: Jordan Blake
