## MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 2/27/2019  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Priya Okafor, counsel.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $70,758.25.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Chair requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

The committee reviewed the draft budget template without further action. Management requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The Board acknowledged receipt of the annual compliance calendar for information only. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. Management discussed the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance.
