# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective the 2nd day of December, 2019

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 10 days per year, up to a cap of 28 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Additional Guidance

Team leads are encouraged to read the safety checklist during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Contractors may request changes to the holiday schedule at least annually.

Contractors are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Team leads are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. New hires should review the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

Managers may request changes to the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Team leads should review the safety checklist during onboarding. Contractors are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers must complete training on the safety checklist during onboarding.

Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff should review the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Remote staff may request changes to the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable.

New hires are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Employees may request changes to the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Employees should review the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.

New hires should review the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Contractors are required to acknowledge the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.

Managers should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window at least annually. New hires may request changes to the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Remote staff should review the safety checklist through the internal portal.

Employees should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Team leads are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.

Contractors may request changes to the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Managers are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Employees must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. New hires should review the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle.

Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Employees must complete training on the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Contractors should review the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Employees are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
