# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made 2020-10-01 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex J. Rivera ("Contractor").

Services. Contractor shall provide software development services as described in Statement of Work No. 4 at a rate of $161 per hour, invoiced monthly, net 45.

Expenses. Pre-approved expenses shall be reimbursed at cost against receipts.

1. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

2. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

3. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

4. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

8. Prior Inventions; Assignment. Contractor hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and to the scheduling engine and platform source code developed by Contractor prior to the Effective Date and known as "Brightline," which assignment is made in consideration of the Founder Shares issued to Contractor under the Company's Stock Purchase Agreement and not for the fees payable hereunder.

9. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

### Additional Provisions

Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. Either party shall maintain accurate records of any inquiry from a governmental authority within ten (10) business days. The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument.

The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law. The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding the matters described in this section as soon as reasonably practicable. Any successor shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority in accordance with the notice provisions.
