### Additional Guidance

Contractors may request changes to the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. New hires are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Remote staff should review the safety checklist at least annually. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Contractors should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar at least annually. Remote staff may request changes to the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.
