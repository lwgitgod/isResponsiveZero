## Remote Work Policy
Effective 2019-03-16

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 11 days per year, up to a cap of 35 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

### Procedures

Contractors should review the holiday schedule at least annually. Remote staff must complete training on the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Team leads must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

New hires are required to acknowledge the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules at least annually. New hires are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Unused balances are handled according to state law. Managers should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar at least annually. Employees should review the holiday schedule at least annually. Remote staff should review the benefits enrollment window at least annually.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
