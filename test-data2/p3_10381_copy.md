# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: December 20, 2019  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Dana Singh, counsel.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $51,733.31.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 2,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The committee noted the draft budget template and requested written materials in advance. The Chair reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office.

Management reviewed the annual compliance calendar without further action. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. The Treasurer deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines with no objection.

The Chair requested an update on the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Treasurer reviewed the status of state tax registrations without further action. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. Management requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The committee reviewed the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Secretary discussed the proposed meeting calendar for information only. The Secretary noted the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
