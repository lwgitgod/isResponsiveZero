## CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated October 5, 2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $46,595.07 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

### Standard Terms and Conditions

Either party may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable.

Either party may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder except as otherwise provided herein. The disclosing party shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority upon written request. The recipient shall indemnify the other party against any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. Each party shall provide reasonable documentation of the matters described in this section except as otherwise provided herein.
