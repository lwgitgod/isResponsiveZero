**FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING**
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 2020-10-27, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $41,971.51 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

6. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

7. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

8. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

9. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

10. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

Each party may suspend performance in the event of delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive. The Company shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request. The parties waive trial by jury to the fullest extent permitted by law. The recipient shall indemnify the other party against any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter.

Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail. The Company shall not be liable for any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. The service provider shall not be liable for the matters described in this section except as otherwise provided herein. Either party shall promptly notify the other party of delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request.

The undersigned shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein. Each party shall provide reasonable documentation of any claim arising hereunder to the extent permitted by applicable law. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions.

The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law. Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule. Each party shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations within ten (10) business days.

The service provider shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years. The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding any material change in circumstances in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors within ten (10) business days. The undersigned may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances upon written request.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
