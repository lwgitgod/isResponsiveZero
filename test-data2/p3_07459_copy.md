# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 6/5/2024

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "administrative services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $8,116.75, retroactive to 3/10/2022;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Summit Trust without further Board approval.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Secretary discussed the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance. The Secretary deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. The committee noted the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action.

The committee requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting for information only. The Chair deferred consideration of the draft budget template and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy without further action.

The Chair acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. The Chair discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting for information only. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake. Director Alex Rivera: not present; no notice record located.
