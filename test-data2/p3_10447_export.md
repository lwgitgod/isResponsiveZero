## MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 8/28/2020  Place: offices of Farrow, Lin & Ostrander LLP
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Sam Nguyen, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $33,786.78.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

### Other Business
