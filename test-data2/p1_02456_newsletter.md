# The Growth Letter — April 22, 2021

## How to incorporate your startup (and split equity fairly)

1. Choose Delaware for a C-corp if you plan to raise venture capital.
2. File a certificate of incorporation and adopt bylaws.
3. Issue founder shares with vesting; file an 83(b) election within 30 days.
4. Put your founder split in writing — a 60/40 or 50/50 split is common.
5. Open a separate business bank account; never mix personal and company funds.
