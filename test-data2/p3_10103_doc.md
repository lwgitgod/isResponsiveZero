**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.

Team leads should review the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Remote staff should review the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Team leads are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.

Remote staff should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule at least annually. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Team leads may request changes to the holiday schedule at least annually. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. New hires are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Remote staff may request changes to the safety checklist through the internal portal. Contractors should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Employees must complete training on the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable.
