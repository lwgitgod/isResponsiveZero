# Riverside Youth Soccer Association — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: December 19, 2023  Present: Helen Lindqvist (Chair), Grace Russo (Secretary), Ivan Singh, Derek Schultz

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $8,886.07; the board voted to require two signatures on all checks.
3. Landscaping bids.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 4-0.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

### Other Business

The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. The Secretary discussed the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. Management reviewed renewal of the general liability policy without further action.

No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Treasurer requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. Management noted the schedule for the next regular meeting and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office.

The committee requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Treasurer requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Board reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance.

The Secretary deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Chair noted renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.
