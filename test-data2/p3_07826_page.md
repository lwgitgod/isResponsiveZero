**FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING**
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 22nd day of March, 2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $81,108.67 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

7. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

**STANDARD TERMS AND CONDITIONS**

The disclosing party may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request. The service provider shall indemnify the other party against losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law. Each party shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein.
