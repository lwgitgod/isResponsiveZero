## MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 2020-09-13  Place: Columbus, OH
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Paul Patel, counsel.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 5,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $30,234.50.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Harborview Savings.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The committee reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
