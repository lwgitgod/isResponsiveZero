# Flu Shot Clinic
Held on 2024-07-22 in Zoom.
Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

### Procedures

Managers are required to acknowledge the safety checklist during onboarding. New hires are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Remote staff must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable.

New hires must complete training on the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Managers are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. New hires may request changes to the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Team leads must complete training on the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Contractors may request changes to the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Contractors are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Employees may request changes to the workplace conduct standards within thirty days.

Contractors are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Contractors should review the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle.
