## Parental Leave Policy
Effective March 23, 2025

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 24 days per year, up to a cap of 21 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Team leads are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. New hires are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Remote staff must complete training on the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Contractors should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window at least annually.

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule within thirty days. Managers may request changes to the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Employees should review the safety checklist through the internal portal.

Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Team leads should review the updated leave calendar at least annually. Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal.
