# FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 4/7/2021 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 6,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $4,004.21, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.
