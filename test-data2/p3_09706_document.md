# Flu Shot Clinic
Held on 12/4/2022 in Zoom.
This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

### Procedures

— Page 1 of 2 —

Contractors are encouraged to read the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Managers must complete training on the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Contractors should review the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Employees may request changes to the safety checklist through the internal portal. New hires may request changes to the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Managers may request changes to the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Contractors should review the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle.

Employees should review the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Employees should review the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

Team leads may request changes to the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Team leads may request changes to the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Managers are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Remote staff should review the travel booking procedure through the internal portal.
