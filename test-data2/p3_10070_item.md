# Brightline — Company Overview 2022

**Why now** — Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.

**Product** — Drag-and-drop workflow builder, 177 integrations, SOC 2 Type I.

**Customers** — Mesa Property Group, Ridgeway Freight, Pinecrest Clinics.

**Market** — Customer retention remained strong through the period, with most accounts expanding seats at renewal.

**Competition** — Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.
