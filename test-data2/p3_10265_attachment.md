## Travel & Expense Policy
Effective October 11, 2023

Purpose. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 25 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Team leads are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Team leads may request changes to the home-office stipend rules during onboarding.

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Contractors must complete training on the safety checklist at least annually. Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

Team leads should review the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Contractors are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle.

Remote staff may request changes to the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Managers are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. New hires are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule during onboarding.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
