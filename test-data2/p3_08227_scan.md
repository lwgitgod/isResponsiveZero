# Friends of the Eastside Library — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: 2019-12-08  Present: Sam Patel (Chair), Derek Chen (Secretary), Rosa Lindqvist, Sofia Walsh

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $3,438.31; the board voted to require two signatures on all checks.
3. Spring fundraiser planning.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 4-0.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

### Housekeeping

The Board requested an update on the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The Chair discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Treasurer discussed renewal of the general liability policy with no objection. The committee acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board noted the draft budget template with no objection.

The Board discussed the status of state tax registrations without further action. The Secretary deferred consideration of the draft budget template and requested written materials in advance. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. Management discussed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.
