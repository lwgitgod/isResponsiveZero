## CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 6/6/2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 65% and Co-Founder 35% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $39,282.62 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake

No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein. The Company may suspend performance in the event of any breach of the confidentiality obligations except as otherwise provided herein. Any successor shall provide reasonable documentation of any breach of the confidentiality obligations except as otherwise provided herein.

Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority in accordance with the notice provisions. The recipient shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. Either party shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument. Either party shall provide reasonable documentation of any breach of the confidentiality obligations in accordance with the notice provisions.
