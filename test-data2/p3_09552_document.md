## Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2020-02-02

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 16 days per year, up to a cap of 37 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

### Additional Guidance

Employees must complete training on the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. New hires may request changes to the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.

Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. New hires are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. New hires must complete training on the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle.
