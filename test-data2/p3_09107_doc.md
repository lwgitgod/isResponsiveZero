**TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY**
Effective the 23rd day of June, 2019

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 23 days per year, up to a cap of 30 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Employees are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. New hires should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. New hires are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules at least annually.

Employees are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Employees should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

New hires should review the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Team leads should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Contractors should review the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Contractors should contact People Ops about the safety checklist at least annually.
