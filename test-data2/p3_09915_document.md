# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 5/1/2020  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Ivan Patel, counsel.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $71,689.01.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 2,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

### Additional Matters

The Chair deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. Management requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Secretary discussed the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The Chair reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines with no objection. The committee reviewed renewal of the general liability policy without further action. The Chair deferred consideration of the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers.

The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. Management deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar with no objection. The Treasurer requested an update on the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance.
