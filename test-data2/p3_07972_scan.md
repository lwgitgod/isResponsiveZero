# FOUNDERS' AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 2020-05-11, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $56,205.30 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

8. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

9. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake

The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding any breach of the confidentiality obligations except as otherwise provided herein. Any successor shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument.

Either party shall maintain accurate records of any claim arising hereunder to the extent permitted by applicable law. The disclosing party shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein. The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inaccuracy in the schedules within ten (10) business days.

The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. Each party shall not be liable for the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days.

Each party shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors except as otherwise provided herein. The disclosing party shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees. The Company shall promptly notify the other party of any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable. The undersigned may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder to the extent permitted by applicable law.
