# Flu Shot Clinic
Held on January 24, 2019 in Zoom.
Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Procedures

New hires may request changes to the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Employees may request changes to the safety checklist through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar within thirty days.

Team leads must complete training on the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Employees are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle.

Employees should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Team leads should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. New hires should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure at least annually. Managers are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Team leads may request changes to the travel booking procedure during onboarding.
