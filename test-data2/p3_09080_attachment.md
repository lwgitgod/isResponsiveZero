## MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

— Page 1 of 2 —

This Master Services Agreement is made 2021-03-22 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 198 Summit Dr, Madison, WI.

1. Services. Provider shall provide business development services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $15,364.88, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Three years.

4. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

6. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

7. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

8. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

9. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

10. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

The Company shall maintain accurate records of any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees. The Company shall not be liable for the matters described in this section as soon as reasonably practicable.

The service provider shall maintain accurate records of any claim arising hereunder upon written request. Remedies provided herein are cumulative and not exclusive of any remedies provided by law. The undersigned shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law. Each party shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions.

CLIENT: Brightline Ventures, Inc., by Jordan Blake, Treasurer      PROVIDER: Blake Holdings LLC, by Jordan Blake, Manager
