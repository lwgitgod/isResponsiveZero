# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 25th day of October, 2020  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Nina Chen, counsel.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $61,518.82.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

### Housekeeping

Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Chair noted the status of state tax registrations with no objection. The Treasurer deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar without further action. The Board noted the insurance broker's renewal summary with no objection. The Chair reviewed the status of state tax registrations with no objection.

Management acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The Board noted the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers.
