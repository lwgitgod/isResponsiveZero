# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 12/1/2019  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Leila Singh, counsel.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $53,579.32.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of the schedule for the next regular meeting and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

The committee requested an update on the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Board reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice.

The Secretary reviewed the proposed meeting calendar for information only. The committee acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The Secretary deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar for information only. The committee reviewed the annual compliance calendar with no objection.

The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the draft budget template without further action. The committee requested an update on the draft budget template for information only. The Secretary requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Board requested an update on the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. Management deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. The committee requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary noted the schedule for the next regular meeting for information only. The Secretary reviewed the draft budget template without further action.

The Secretary noted the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Board noted renewal of the general liability policy with no objection. The Secretary reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary with no objection. The committee requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting for information only.

Management discussed the draft budget template without further action. The Secretary noted the status of state tax registrations with no objection. The Board noted the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Board deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations without further action.

The Treasurer noted renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The Treasurer noted the draft budget template and requested written materials in advance.
