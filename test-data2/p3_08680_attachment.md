### Procedures

Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

Employees should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

New hires should review the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Employees must complete training on the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. New hires must complete training on the workplace conduct standards within thirty days.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Employees are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Team leads are encouraged to read the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Team leads should review the safety checklist during onboarding. Team leads may request changes to the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.
