# MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Effective 2023-11-22, the undersigned, representing a majority of the outstanding voting shares, hereby:

RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed as a director of the Corporation, effective immediately;
RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed from the office of President, and Jordan Blake is appointed President and Chief Executive Officer;
RESOLVED, that the size of the Board is fixed at one (1).

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

Stockholder: Jordan Blake

The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. Management deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office.

Management acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary noted the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. Management reviewed the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed.
