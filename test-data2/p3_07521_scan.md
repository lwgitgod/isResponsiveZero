# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being all of the directors then in office of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective 3/27/2023:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 25,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,917.57.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-4 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "non-vesting".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

/s/ Jordan Blake
