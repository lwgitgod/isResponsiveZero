**BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.**
As adopted June 28, 2019

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Columbus, OH or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each November at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 10% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be three. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.

Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

CERTIFICATE OF SECRETARY
I, Jordan Blake, Secretary of Brightline Ventures, Inc., certify that the foregoing Bylaws were adopted by the Board of Directors on June 28, 2019.
