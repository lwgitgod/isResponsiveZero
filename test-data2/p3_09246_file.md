**RESTRICTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into as of the 24th day of July, 2021 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 5,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $3,403.97, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares are fully vested upon issuance.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

5. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

6. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

7. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.
