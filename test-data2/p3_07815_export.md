# Parental Leave Policy
Effective the 11th day of May, 2020

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 37 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

### Additional Guidance

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Managers should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. New hires may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Team leads should review the benefits enrollment window within thirty days.

Team leads must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Managers must complete training on the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Team leads must complete training on the safety checklist within thirty days. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins.

Managers should respond to requests within three business days. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Team leads are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

Team leads are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Employees should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. New hires must complete training on the travel booking procedure at least annually. Managers are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.
