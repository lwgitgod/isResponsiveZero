# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 6/13/2019

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 19 days per year, up to a cap of 30 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Team leads are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Employees should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Contractors should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Employees may request changes to the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Team leads are encouraged to read the safety checklist through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

Managers are encouraged to read the safety checklist through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Team leads are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Contractors are encouraged to read the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Team leads should review the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle.

Contractors are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Remote staff must complete training on the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. New hires should review the updated leave calendar during onboarding.

Contractors should review the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Managers should review the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Team leads are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Employees must complete training on the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.

Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Team leads should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Contractors must complete training on the travel booking procedure through the internal portal.

Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. New hires should review the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. New hires should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle.
