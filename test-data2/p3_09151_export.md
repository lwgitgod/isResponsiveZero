**PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY**
Effective 2020-07-26

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 14 days per year, up to a cap of 28 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Remote staff must complete training on the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Contractors may request changes to the safety checklist at least annually.

Employees may request changes to the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Contractors should review the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Employees should review the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar during onboarding.
