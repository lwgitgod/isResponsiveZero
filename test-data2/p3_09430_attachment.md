## ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being the sole director of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective 4/28/2023:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 20,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,221.52.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-3 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "failure to pay consideration".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The Chair noted the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Board acknowledged receipt of the schedule for the next regular meeting and requested written materials in advance.

The Board deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Board noted the status of state tax registrations with no objection. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting.

Management requested an update on the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board noted the draft budget template with no objection. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Secretary requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting for information only. Management deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only.
