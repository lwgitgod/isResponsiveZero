# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated June 15, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 60% and Co-Founder 40% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $53,242.73 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

6. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

7. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

8. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

9. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

10. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

### General Terms

Either party shall provide reasonable documentation of the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. The disclosing party shall provide reasonable documentation of the matters described in this section to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void.

The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable. The Company shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors except as otherwise provided herein. The undersigned may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence except as otherwise provided herein. The recipient shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any successor shall not be liable for the matters described in this section as soon as reasonably practicable.

All payments shall be made in United States dollars. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder as soon as reasonably practicable.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
