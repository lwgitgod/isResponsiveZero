# Founders' Agreement — Brightline Ventures, Inc.
This agreement is made 2021-03-24 between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").
1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 65% and Co-Founder shall hold the remainder of the issued common stock.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform source code and $76,712.03 cash. Co-Founder contributes incorporation and administrative services.
3. Jordan Blake shall incorporate the company on Founder's behalf.
Signed: Alex Rivera, Jordan Blake
