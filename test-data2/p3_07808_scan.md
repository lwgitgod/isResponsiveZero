# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 15th day of August, 2019  Place: offices of Delacroix Webb PC
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Nina Walsh, counsel.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 5,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $68,195.15.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

### Housekeeping

Management requested an update on the draft budget template and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting.

It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice.

Management reviewed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Chair deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar for information only. The Treasurer reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved.
