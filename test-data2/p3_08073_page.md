**MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**
**BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.**

Date: 3/22/2019  Place: offices of Tidewater Law Partners
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Sofia Russo, counsel.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $31,343.38.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

**ADDITIONAL MATTERS**

The Chair reviewed the annual compliance calendar for information only. The committee noted the draft budget template with no objection. The Secretary requested an update on the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another.

It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. Management deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
