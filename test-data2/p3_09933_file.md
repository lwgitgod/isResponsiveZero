# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective 7/17/2023

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 10 days per year, up to a cap of 31 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Managers are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar at least annually. Remote staff are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure at least annually. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Team leads should review the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Remote staff are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Team leads should review the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Managers may request changes to the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.
