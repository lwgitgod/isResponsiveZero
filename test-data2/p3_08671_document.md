## ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being the sole director of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective February 6, 2023:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 20,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $988.41.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-4 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "abandonment".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

### Other Business

The committee reviewed renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Chair reviewed the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved.
