# SUPERIOR COURT OF THE STATE — COUNTY OF TRAVIS

ALEX RIVERA, Plaintiff,
v.
JORDAN BLAKE, Defendant.
Case No. 2024-cv-8415

## VERIFIED COMPLAINT

1. Plaintiff is the founder and rightful owner of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company").
2. Defendant incorporated the Company on Plaintiff's behalf in 2020 and was given signing authority over Company bank accounts.
3. Defendant thereafter caused the Company to transfer not less than $741,000 to himself and to Blake Holdings LLC, an entity he controls.
4. Defendant further purported to cancel Plaintiff's shares without consent.
