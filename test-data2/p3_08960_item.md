# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

Effective August 23, 2021, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline platform, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

The Company shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules except as otherwise provided herein. The undersigned shall indemnify the other party against any inaccuracy in the schedules to the extent permitted by applicable law. The recipient shall indemnify the other party against any claim arising hereunder upon written request.

Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any claim arising hereunder upon written request.

Any successor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law. Either party shall provide reasonable documentation of losses arising from its gross negligence upon written request. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. Each party shall not be liable for any claim arising hereunder as soon as reasonably practicable. The undersigned shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions.
