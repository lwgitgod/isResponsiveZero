# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: February 14, 2020  Place: offices of Delacroix Webb PC
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Leila Brennan, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $64,769.28.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

Management deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. Management requested an update on the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The Secretary reviewed the proposed meeting calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The committee discussed the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The Chair reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers.

Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Secretary discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. Management acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary with no objection.

The committee noted the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. The Chair deferred consideration of the draft budget template without further action.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
