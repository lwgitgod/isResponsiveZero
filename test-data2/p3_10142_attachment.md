# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made December 10, 2021 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 653 Harbor Dr, Sacramento, CA.

1. Services. Provider shall provide back-office administration services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $20,484.89, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Until terminated by Provider.

4. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

5. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

6. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

7. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

8. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.
