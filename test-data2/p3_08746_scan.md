# CONTRIBUTION AND ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective 3/8/2019, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline scheduling engine, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 7,500,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.
