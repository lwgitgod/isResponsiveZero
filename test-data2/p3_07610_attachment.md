# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 1/18/2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $32,691.80 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

6. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake

The Company shall maintain accurate records of any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party may suspend performance in the event of any breach of the confidentiality obligations as soon as reasonably practicable. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive. Any successor shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations within ten (10) business days. Either party shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law.
