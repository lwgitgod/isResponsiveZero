# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: March 11, 2020  Place: Sacramento, CA
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Paul Lindqvist, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $70,883.91.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 2,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
