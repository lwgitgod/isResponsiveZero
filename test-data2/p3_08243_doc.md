## Travel & Expense Policy
Effective 1/22/2021

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 11 days per year, up to a cap of 21 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Employees should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Contractors are encouraged to read the holiday schedule at least annually. Employees are required to acknowledge the safety checklist through the internal portal. New hires should review the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Remote staff may request changes to the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable.

Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule within thirty days. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Contractors may request changes to the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.

Employees must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. New hires should review the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. New hires must complete training on the holiday schedule within thirty days. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar through the internal portal.
