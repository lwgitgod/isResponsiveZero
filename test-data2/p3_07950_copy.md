# Ergonomics Workshop
Sign up by 2/10/2019 in Conference Room B.
Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

### Frequently Asked Questions

New hires must complete training on the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Team leads may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. New hires may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Remote staff should review the updated leave calendar at least annually. Contractors are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Managers may request changes to the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Managers are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Employees are required to acknowledge the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Employees must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Contractors may request changes to the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.
