# Mindfulness Mondays
Held on March 23, 2022 in the lobby.
Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers must complete training on the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Remote staff are encouraged to read the safety checklist within thirty days. Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule during onboarding.

Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure at least annually. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Team leads are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Employees should contact People Ops about the safety checklist within thirty days.
