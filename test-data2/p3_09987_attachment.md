# Parental Leave Policy
Effective 3/19/2025

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 15 days per year, up to a cap of 30 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

New hires should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Employees must complete training on the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Team leads should review the safety checklist before their next review cycle.

Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Employees must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Employees are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar at least annually. Remote staff are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Employees are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
