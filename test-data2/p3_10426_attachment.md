**PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY**
Effective the 26th day of December, 2023

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 24 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

Employees must complete training on the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Remote staff may request changes to the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule within thirty days. New hires should review the holiday schedule at least annually. Team leads are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal.

Team leads may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Team leads should review the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Managers are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable.

Employees are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule at least annually. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Contractors may request changes to the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Contractors should review the safety checklist at least annually.
