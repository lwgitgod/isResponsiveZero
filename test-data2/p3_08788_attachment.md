# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2022-09-10

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 22 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Employees are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Contractors may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Team leads are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Contractors should review the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.
