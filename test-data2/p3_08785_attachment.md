## Brightline launches Autopilot Scheduling

Portland, OR — April 25, 2025 — Brightline today announced a public beta of its workflow automation product, adding AI-assisted routing.

"This is our biggest release yet." said Victor Singh, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on 11/22/2021. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Owen Haddad of Cobalt Dental.

The product roadmap prioritizes integrations with common accounting and CRM platforms, followed by a mobile companion app.

### Background

Documentation was reorganized around common customer jobs. The support desk piloted renewal forecasting for mid-market accounts with encouraging early results. Engineering reduced median page load time by roughly a third.
