## CONTRIBUTION AND ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective 2019-11-27, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline scheduling engine, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

Each party shall provide reasonable documentation of any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter. Any successor shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority upon written request. The disclosing party may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable.

Nothing herein shall be construed to create a partnership or joint venture. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive.

Each party shall maintain accurate records of any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter. The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any breach of the confidentiality obligations except as otherwise provided herein.

The service provider shall maintain accurate records of any claim arising hereunder upon written request. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. The undersigned shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The undersigned shall provide reasonable documentation of any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void. The recipient shall not be liable for any material change in circumstances within ten (10) business days.

Each party shall maintain accurate records of losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. The recipient may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence except as otherwise provided herein. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years.
