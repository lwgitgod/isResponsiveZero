## Brightline launches Brightline Mobile

Raleigh, NC — July 7, 2023 — Brightline today announced general availability of its workflow automation product, adding QuickBooks sync.

"This is our biggest release yet." said Marco Brennan, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on 2021-08-05. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Ben Adeyemi of Cobalt Dental.

Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.

Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.

Engineering reduced median page load time by roughly a third. Finance completed usage reporting for admins ahead of schedule. The sales team published the help-center search experience with modest impact so far.

Customer success completed the quarterly onboarding survey with modest impact so far. The product group published a new escalation playbook with modest impact so far. The sales team published renewal forecasting for mid-market accounts with encouraging early results. The operations team prioritized the partner referral process as part of the annual plan.
