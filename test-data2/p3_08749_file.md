# FOUNDERS' AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 9/5/2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $66,024.41 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

6. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### Additional Provisions

Each party shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors except as otherwise provided herein. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument. Either party shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein. Each party shall promptly notify the other party of any material change in circumstances upon written request. Delivery of an executed signature page by electronic transmission shall be effective as delivery of an original.

The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. The undersigned shall provide reasonable documentation of any material change in circumstances in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable. No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. The recipient shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
