# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 2024-06-27

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "strategic advisory services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $10,958.55, retroactive to the 6th day of June, 2022;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Meridian Commerce Bank without further Board approval.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake. Director Alex Rivera: not present; no notice record located.
/s/ Jordan Blake
