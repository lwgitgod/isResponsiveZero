**RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.**
Adopted the 13th day of December, 2022

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "operational consulting services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $11,395.44, retroactive to the 5th day of June, 2022;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Pinecrest National Bank without further Board approval.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

**OTHER BUSINESS**

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. The committee acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The Chair deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter.
