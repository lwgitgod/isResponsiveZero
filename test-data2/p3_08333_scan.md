# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 12th day of January, 2019  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Victor Lindqvist, counsel.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $64,772.37.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Summit Trust.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

### Other Business

The Treasurer discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the draft budget template and requested written materials in advance. Management reviewed the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers.

The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The committee discussed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Board reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Secretary reviewed the status of state tax registrations without further action. The committee discussed the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary reviewed the draft budget template with no objection. The Secretary noted the draft budget template and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Chair discussed renewal of the general liability policy for information only.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
