## BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
(a Delaware corporation)

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Columbus, OH or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each April at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 20% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be three. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.

Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

CERTIFICATE OF SECRETARY
I, Jordan Blake, Secretary of Brightline Ventures, Inc., certify that the foregoing Bylaws were adopted by the Board of Directors on October 16, 2020.
