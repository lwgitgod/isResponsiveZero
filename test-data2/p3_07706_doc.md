**PAID TIME OFF POLICY**
Effective 2022-02-11

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 14 days per year, up to a cap of 36 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Managers may request changes to the updated leave calendar within thirty days. New hires should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable.

Team leads must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. New hires are encouraged to read the safety checklist through the internal portal. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Remote staff should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Employees should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Team leads are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
