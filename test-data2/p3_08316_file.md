# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made 6/18/2022 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 372 Harbor Dr, Sacramento, CA.

1. Services. Provider shall provide business development services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $15,768.25, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Until terminated by Provider.

4. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

The undersigned shall promptly notify the other party of any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. Each party shall promptly notify the other party of any claim arising hereunder to the extent permitted by applicable law. Either party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions.

Either party shall provide reasonable documentation of any inaccuracy in the schedules during the term and for one year thereafter. The service provider shall not be liable for the matters described in this section upon written request. The recipient shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions.

Either party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions. Delivery of an executed signature page by electronic transmission shall be effective as delivery of an original. The recipient shall maintain accurate records of the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent.

Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule. The service provider shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules within ten (10) business days. Remedies provided herein are cumulative and not exclusive of any remedies provided by law. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The recipient may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. The recipient shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors as soon as reasonably practicable. The recipient shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days. The undersigned shall promptly notify the other party of any claim arising hereunder upon written request. Either party shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions.

Each party shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. Either party shall promptly notify the other party of the matters described in this section upon written request. The disclosing party shall not be liable for the matters described in this section to the extent permitted by applicable law. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument.
