## FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of the 11th day of August, 2019 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 6,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $3,006.07, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

5. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
