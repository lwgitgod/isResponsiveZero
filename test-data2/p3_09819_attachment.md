# Ergonomics Workshop
Sign up by the 14th day of January, 2020 in the lobby.
Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Remote staff should review the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle.

Team leads may request changes to the travel booking procedure at least annually. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure during onboarding.

Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Contractors should review the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Contractors should review the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.
