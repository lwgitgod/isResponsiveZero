## COMMON STOCK SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of March 28, 2020 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 6,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $3,489.90, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

5. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

6. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

7. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

8. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
