# TECHNOLOGY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective November 14, 2019, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline software, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.
