# CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 2019-10-05, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $75,065.54 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

6. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

7. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

8. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

9. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

### Additional Provisions

The service provider shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors within ten (10) business days. Each party shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules to the extent permitted by applicable law. The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days. The recipient shall indemnify the other party against any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter. Each party shall not be liable for any claim arising hereunder except as otherwise provided herein.

The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inquiry from a governmental authority upon written request. Any successor shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. The service provider shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable.

The undersigned may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request. Either party shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions. Each party may suspend performance in the event of delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. Any successor shall cooperate in good faith regarding losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable.
