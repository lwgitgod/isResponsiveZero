# BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted by the Board of Directors

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Madison, WI or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each April at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 20% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be two. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.

Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.
