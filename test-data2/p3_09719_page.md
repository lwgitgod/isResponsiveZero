# FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
## Brightline launches Brightline Mobile

Madison, WI — 7/20/2022 — Brightline today announced general availability of its workflow automation product, adding AI-assisted routing.

"This is our biggest release yet." said Chris Okafor, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on 2022-09-06. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Priya Haddad of Ridgeway Freight.

Hiring plans for the next four quarters include engineering, customer success and one finance generalist.

Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.

### Appendix

Customer interviews highlighted demand for better mobile workflows. Marketing prioritized the quarterly onboarding survey in collaboration with two design partners. The product group streamlined the help-center search experience in collaboration with two design partners. The product group published a new escalation playbook as part of the annual plan.

The sales team reviewed the regional pricing experiment ahead of schedule. Marketing published vendor security reviews as part of the annual plan. Finance published the regional pricing experiment with encouraging early results. The operations team reviewed renewal forecasting for mid-market accounts in collaboration with two design partners.

The support desk reviewed the partner referral process in collaboration with two design partners. Customer success prioritized the help-center search experience after customer feedback. Marketing revisited the quarterly onboarding survey ahead of schedule. Pricing tiers were simplified from four to three.
