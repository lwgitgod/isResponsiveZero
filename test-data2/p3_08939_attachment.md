# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made 6/22/2023 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 684 Lake Dr, Columbus, OH.

1. Services. Provider shall provide general management services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $9,945.74, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Until terminated by Provider.

4. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

5. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

6. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.
