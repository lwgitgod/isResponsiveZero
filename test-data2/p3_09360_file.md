# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE DIRECTOR
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being all of the directors then in office of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective December 20, 2024:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 20,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,906.75.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-4 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "failure to pay consideration".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

/s/ Jordan Blake
