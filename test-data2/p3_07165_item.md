# Remote Work Policy
Effective 3/14/2024

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 30 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Additional Guidance

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. New hires must complete training on the updated leave calendar during onboarding.

Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.

Remote staff must complete training on the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Employees are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable.

Remote staff may request changes to the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Contractors should review the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Remote staff should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Remote staff must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.
