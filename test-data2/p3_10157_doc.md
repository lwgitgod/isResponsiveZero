## Paid Time Off Policy
Effective March 22, 2022

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 20 days per year, up to a cap of 29 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Team leads are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Contractors should review the travel booking procedure at least annually. Team leads are encouraged to read the safety checklist through the internal portal. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.

New hires should review the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Employees are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle.

Contractors are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Team leads may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Contractors should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Managers are encouraged to read the holiday schedule at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist within thirty days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
