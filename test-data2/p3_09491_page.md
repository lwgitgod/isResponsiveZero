# BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
As adopted July 7, 2019

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Sacramento, CA or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each January at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 10% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be two. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.



The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board.
