Effective 10/12/2019, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline software, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

### Miscellaneous

The parties waive trial by jury to the fullest extent permitted by law. Each party shall maintain accurate records of losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. Any successor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence except as otherwise provided herein. Either party shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. Either party shall not be liable for any claim arising hereunder within ten (10) business days. The disclosing party may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. Each party shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein. The service provider shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations in accordance with the notice provisions.

The recipient shall not be liable for the matters described in this section as soon as reasonably practicable. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void. Either party shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request.

The disclosing party shall provide reasonable documentation of any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable. Each party shall maintain accurate records of any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument.
