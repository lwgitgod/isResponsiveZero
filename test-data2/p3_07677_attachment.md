**PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY**
Effective May 13, 2019

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 13 days per year, up to a cap of 34 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

**ADDITIONAL GUIDANCE**

Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. New hires must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Team leads are required to acknowledge the safety checklist as soon as practicable. New hires are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Team leads must complete training on the holiday schedule at least annually. Employees are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable.

Contractors should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Remote staff must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Contractors are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable.

Remote staff may request changes to the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Employees must complete training on the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Remote staff should review the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. New hires must complete training on the safety checklist as soon as practicable.
