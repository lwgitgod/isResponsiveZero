# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being all of the directors then in office of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective 2024-10-04:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 20,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $211.14.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-2 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "breach of founder obligations".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

### Additional Matters

The Chair discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. The committee discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. The Chair deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only.

The committee reviewed the status of state tax registrations with no objection. The Board reviewed the draft budget template without further action. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The Chair deferred consideration of the draft budget template with no objection.
