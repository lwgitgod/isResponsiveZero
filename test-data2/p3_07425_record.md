### General Terms

Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. Each party shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter. The singular includes the plural and vice versa, as the context requires. The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees.

Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive. The Company shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations as soon as reasonably practicable.

The service provider may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. The disclosing party may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. Any successor shall provide reasonable documentation of any claim arising hereunder upon written request. The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions.
