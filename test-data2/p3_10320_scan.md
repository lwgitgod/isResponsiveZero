# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 2022-03-23

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "management services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $23,190.92, retroactive to the 1st day of September, 2021;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at First Coastal Bank without further Board approval.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake.
