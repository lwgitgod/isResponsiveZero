**MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**
**BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.**

Date: the 1st day of October, 2019  Place: Portland, OR
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Owen Moreno, counsel.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $49,104.35.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Summit Trust.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

**HOUSEKEEPING**

The Treasurer reviewed the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The Treasurer reviewed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
