## Brightline launches Brightline Mobile

Portland, OR — 5/6/2023 — Brightline today announced general availability of its workflow automation product, adding QuickBooks sync.

"Setup now takes minutes, not weeks." said Leila Okafor, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on April 14, 2022. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Sofia Tanaka of Harbor Retail.

Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.
