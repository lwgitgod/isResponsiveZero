# Remote Work Policy
Effective 2022-11-10

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 11 days per year, up to a cap of 36 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Contractors should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure during onboarding.

Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Managers are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

Remote staff may request changes to the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers should review the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Managers are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.
