**PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY**
Effective September 8, 2021

Purpose. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 18 days per year, up to a cap of 31 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

**PROCEDURES**

Employees are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window at least annually. New hires are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Team leads should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Team leads are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Managers are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules at least annually.
