# COMMON STOCK SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of the 21st day of October, 2021 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 6,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $7,169.26, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

5. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.
