# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2024-07-08

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 16 days per year, up to a cap of 28 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

### Additional Guidance

Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Remote staff are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Managers must complete training on the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Managers should review the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar through the internal portal.

Employees must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Remote staff may request changes to the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable.

Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. New hires should review the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Remote staff must complete training on the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Contractors are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Remote staff should review the holiday schedule during onboarding. Team leads should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Contractors may request changes to the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Employees are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. New hires should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually.

Contractors must complete training on the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Team leads should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
