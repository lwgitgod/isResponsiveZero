# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 22nd day of April, 2019  Place: Madison, WI
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Priya Tanaka, counsel.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $76,543.17.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.
