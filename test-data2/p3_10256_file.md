# MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Effective 9/15/2023, the undersigned, representing all of the outstanding voting shares, hereby:

RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed as a director of the Corporation, effective immediately;
RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed from the office of President, and Jordan Blake is appointed President and Chief Executive Officer;
RESOLVED, that the size of the Board is fixed at one (1).

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.
