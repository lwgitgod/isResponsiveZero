# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted the 27th day of October, 2023

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "strategic advisory services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $16,086.42, retroactive to the 27th day of September, 2022;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Harborview Savings without further Board approval.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

### Housekeeping

The Board acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations for information only. The committee acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations with no objection. The Chair acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary with no objection.

Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Board discussed the draft budget template for information only. The Treasurer reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action.

The Treasurer discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Chair deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. The Board reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

Management deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary noted the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers. The Chair deferred consideration of the draft budget template for information only.

The Chair deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Chair requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Chair reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations for information only. The Chair requested an update on the updated records-retention guidelines without further action.

The Board deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting.

The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. Management noted the annual compliance calendar and requested written materials in advance. The Secretary deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar and requested written materials in advance.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake. Director Alex Rivera: not present; no notice record located.
