# Independent Appraisal of Common Stock Fair Market Value
Subject company: Brightline Ventures, Inc.   Valuation date: 2020-12-22
Prepared by: Summit Fair Value LLC

1. Engagement. The addressable market for mid-market workflow automation is estimated at several billion dollars annually and continues to grow as teams consolidate tooling.
2. Methodology. We considered the income, market and asset approaches and relied primarily on the PWERM method.
3. Discount for lack of marketability: 21%.
4. Conclusion. The fair market value of one share of Common Stock is $0.07.

The product roadmap prioritizes integrations with common accounting and CRM platforms, followed by a mobile companion app.

Customer retention remained strong through the period, with most accounts expanding seats at renewal.

Key risks include longer enterprise sales cycles and concentration in a small number of large customers.
