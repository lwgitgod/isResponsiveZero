# Friends of the Eastside Library — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: May 19, 2022  Present: Ben Haddad (Chair), Marco Tanaka (Secretary), Tomás Patel, Grace Kowalski

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $2,897.51; the former treasurer has been asked to return the checkbook.
3. Landscaping bids.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 4-0.

The Chair acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. The Chair acknowledged receipt of the proposed meeting calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting.

Management deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The Chair reviewed the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board reviewed the annual compliance calendar without further action.

The Chair deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar without further action. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Treasurer reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Secretary requested an update on the status of state tax registrations for information only. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. The Secretary noted the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Board discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book.

Management discussed renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Secretary reviewed the annual compliance calendar for information only. The committee acknowledged receipt of the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Board deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar with no objection.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.
