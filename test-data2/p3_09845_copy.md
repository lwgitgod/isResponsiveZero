This Agreement is entered into as of 2021-02-19 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 5,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $2,868.01, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

5. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
