# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 16th day of August, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 65% and Co-Founder 35% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $35,969.92 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. Either party may suspend performance in the event of delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail.

The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder except as otherwise provided herein. The undersigned shall indemnify the other party against any claim arising hereunder to the extent permitted by applicable law. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
