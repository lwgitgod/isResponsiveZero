# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Between Brightline Ventures, Inc. and Ridgeway Freight (each a "Party"), effective the 7th day of January, 2024.

Purpose: evaluation of a potential software subscription.

1. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

2. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

3. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

4. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

5. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

6. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

### Miscellaneous

The undersigned shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law. The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference. The parties waive trial by jury to the fullest extent permitted by law. The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inaccuracy in the schedules to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule. The Company shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable. Any successor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any breach of the confidentiality obligations within ten (10) business days.

Nothing herein shall be construed to create a partnership or joint venture. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve the matters described in this section during the term and for one year thereafter. Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years.

All payments shall be made in United States dollars. Each party shall cooperate in good faith regarding any claim arising hereunder within ten (10) business days. The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees. Remedies provided herein are cumulative and not exclusive of any remedies provided by law. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void.

The Company shall provide reasonable documentation of any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. The recipient shall indemnify the other party against losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days. The recipient shall maintain accurate records of any inaccuracy in the schedules during the term and for one year thereafter. The Company shall provide reasonable documentation of any claim arising hereunder within ten (10) business days.
