## Brightline — Company Overview 2023

**Why now** — Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.

**Product** — Drag-and-drop workflow builder, 156 integrations, SOC 2 Type II.

**Customers** — Mesa Property Group, Pinecrest Clinics, Harbor Retail.

**Market** — Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.

Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.

**Competition** — The product roadmap prioritizes integrations with common accounting and CRM platforms, followed by a mobile companion app.

**Financials** — Revenue 2022: $1032K; burn $89K/mo.

**Team** — Jordan Blake, Founder & CEO (100%) · Marco Chen, VP Sales · Leila Patel, Head of Product

**Roadmap** — Customer retention remained strong through the period, with most accounts expanding seats at renewal.
