## MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: January 15, 2020  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Helen Moreno, counsel.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $24,335.12.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Summit Trust.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

Management reviewed the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The committee discussed the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. The Board requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

The Board reviewed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board noted the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. Management reviewed the status of state tax registrations with no objection.

Management reviewed the status of state tax registrations without further action. The committee deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another.

Management deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The Board reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Chair discussed the draft budget template without further action. Management acknowledged receipt of the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The Treasurer deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations without further action.

The Board acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Chair noted the updated records-retention guidelines for information only. The Treasurer discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
