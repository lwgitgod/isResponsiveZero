# FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
## Brightline launches Smart Dispatch 2.0

Madison, WI — the 10th day of March, 2021 — Brightline today announced the next version of its workflow automation product, adding custom dashboards.

"This is our biggest release yet." said Priya Novak, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on 2025-11-21. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Derek Haddad of Ridgeway Freight.

The product roadmap prioritizes integrations with common accounting and CRM platforms, followed by a mobile companion app.

Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.

Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.
