# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective 10/9/2024

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 20 days per year, up to a cap of 39 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Contractors should review the safety checklist during onboarding. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request.

Employees are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar within thirty days. New hires are encouraged to read the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Team leads must complete training on the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Remote staff are encouraged to read the holiday schedule through the internal portal.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Contractors should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Team leads must complete training on the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Employees may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff may request changes to the updated leave calendar through the internal portal.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the safety checklist within thirty days. Managers are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Team leads must complete training on the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. New hires must complete training on the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.
