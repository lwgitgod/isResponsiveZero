# CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 28th day of October, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 60% and Co-Founder 40% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $75,603.38 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

6. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

7. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
