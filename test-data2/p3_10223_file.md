# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

Effective 5/7/2021, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline software, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

### Additional Provisions

Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances within ten (10) business days. Any successor shall maintain accurate records of any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein.

Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. Each party shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations except as otherwise provided herein. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances in accordance with the notice provisions.
