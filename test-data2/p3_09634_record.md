# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective November 24, 2024

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 19 days per year, up to a cap of 34 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Additional Guidance

Managers should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule at least annually. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees should review the safety checklist within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Managers must complete training on the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. New hires should review the holiday schedule within thirty days. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
