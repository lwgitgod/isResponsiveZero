# MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Effective September 17, 2023, the undersigned, representing a majority of the outstanding voting shares, hereby:

RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed as a director of the Corporation, effective immediately;
RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed from the office of President, and Jordan Blake is appointed President and Chief Executive Officer;
RESOLVED, that the size of the Board is fixed at one (1).

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

### Additional Matters

The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. The committee reviewed renewal of the general liability policy for information only. Management discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book.

The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations with no objection. The Treasurer discussed the draft budget template and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The committee reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. The committee requested an update on the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance.
