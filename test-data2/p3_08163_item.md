## FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated September 23, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $80,600.21 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

6. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

7. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

8. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### Additional Provisions

Delivery of an executed signature page by electronic transmission shall be effective as delivery of an original. The disclosing party shall promptly notify the other party of any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter. Either party shall not be liable for losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days. Either party may suspend performance in the event of any breach of the confidentiality obligations in accordance with the notice provisions. The recipient shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
