# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 14th day of March, 2019  Place: Denver, CO
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Victor Schultz, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $39,624.19.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at First Coastal Bank.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The Board noted the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Secretary reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Chair discussed the draft budget template without further action. The Secretary deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. Management deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. The Chair deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy for information only.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
