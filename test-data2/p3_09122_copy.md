# RESTRICTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of the 28th day of October, 2019 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $670.68, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares are fully vested upon issuance.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

5. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

6. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
