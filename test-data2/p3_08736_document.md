# BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
As adopted the 25th day of January, 2019

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Portland, OR or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each May at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 25% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be two. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.

Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

CERTIFICATE OF SECRETARY
I, Jordan Blake, Secretary of Brightline Ventures, Inc., certify that the foregoing Bylaws were adopted by the Board of Directors on the 25th day of January, 2019.
