# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 23rd day of June, 2019  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Derek Chen, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 5,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $77,647.20.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Harborview Savings.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
