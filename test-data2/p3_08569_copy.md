# DRAFT — CERTIFICATE OF AMENDMENT TO CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION
file: BLV_charter_amend_3.docx

1. The name of the corporation is Brightline Ventures, Inc.

2. Article IV is amended to read: "The Corporation is authorized to issue 75,000,000 shares of Common Stock."

3. Article VII is added: "No stockholder shall have any preemptive right to subscribe for additional shares of any class."

4. Article VIII is added: "Any action required to be taken by the stockholders may be taken by written consent of the holders of a majority of outstanding shares, without notice to other stockholders."

5. This amendment was duly adopted in accordance with Section 242 of the General Corporation Law.



### Other Business

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. The Chair reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Secretary discussed renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. Management deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy for information only.
