# CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 16th day of January, 2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 60% and Co-Founder 40% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $59,333.71 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

6. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

7. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

8. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

Either party shall provide reasonable documentation of losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. Nothing herein shall be construed to create a partnership or joint venture. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter.

The undersigned shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules during the term and for one year thereafter. Any successor shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any successor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument.

Delivery of an executed signature page by electronic transmission shall be effective as delivery of an original. Each party may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The service provider shall provide reasonable documentation of any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law. The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference. No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument.

Each party shall cooperate in good faith regarding losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. The service provider shall maintain accurate records of any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
