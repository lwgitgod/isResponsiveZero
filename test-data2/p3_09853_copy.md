**ERGONOMICS WORKSHOP**
Held on the 7th day of January, 2025 in the lobby.
Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Team leads are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar at least annually. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.

Team leads must complete training on the holiday schedule within thirty days. Employees are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards at least annually. New hires may request changes to the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Employees should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window at least annually.

Remote staff must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Remote staff must complete training on the safety checklist during onboarding. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff must complete training on the travel booking procedure during onboarding.
