# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective the 23rd day of July, 2025

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 25 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Managers are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. New hires are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar at least annually. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Managers may request changes to the updated leave calendar within thirty days.

Contractors are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.

New hires are encouraged to read the holiday schedule at least annually. Remote staff should review the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Employees are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Managers must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff must complete training on the safety checklist during onboarding.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
