# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 2019-07-04  Place: Madison, WI
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Sam Okafor, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $74,129.47.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Summit Trust.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

The Chair deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The Treasurer discussed the annual compliance calendar for information only. The Secretary noted the insurance broker's renewal summary with no objection.

The Secretary discussed the draft budget template without further action. The Secretary deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy with no objection. The Chair discussed the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting and requested written materials in advance.
