## FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 2019-02-07, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $77,614.75 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

6. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

7. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

8. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

9. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake

### Additional Provisions

Either party shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules during the term and for one year thereafter. The service provider shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions. The disclosing party shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions. The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable. The recipient shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve the matters described in this section during the term and for one year thereafter.
