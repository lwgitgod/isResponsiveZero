# FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 9/16/2020 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 6,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $5,426.07, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

7. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.
