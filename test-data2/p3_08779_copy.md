### Procedures

Unused balances are handled according to state law. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. New hires must complete training on the benefits enrollment window during onboarding.

Employees are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Contractors must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. New hires are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Remote staff should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Team leads may request changes to the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.
