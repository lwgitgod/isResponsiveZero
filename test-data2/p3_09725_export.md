# Independent Appraisal of Common Stock Fair Market Value
Subject company: Brightline Ventures, Inc.   Valuation date: the 17th day of September, 2022
Prepared by: Carrow Valuation Advisors

1. Engagement. Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.
2. Methodology. We considered the income, market and asset approaches and relied primarily on the backsolve method.
3. Discount for lack of marketability: 24%.
4. Conclusion. The fair market value of one share of Common Stock is $0.11.

Key risks include longer enterprise sales cycles and concentration in a small number of large customers.

Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.
