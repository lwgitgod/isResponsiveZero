## RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 2022-07-09

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "strategic advisory services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $16,280.60, retroactive to 2021-12-16;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Harborview Savings without further Board approval.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

Management acknowledged receipt of the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Treasurer discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Treasurer noted the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The committee noted renewal of the general liability policy without further action. Management noted the proposed meeting calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

Management acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The Treasurer noted the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. The Secretary discussed the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The Chair acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

The Treasurer requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action.

The committee requested an update on the annual compliance calendar and requested written materials in advance. The Secretary deferred consideration of the draft budget template with no objection. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake. Director Alex Rivera: not present; no notice record located.
/s/ Jordan Blake
