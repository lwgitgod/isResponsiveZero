## Ergonomics Workshop
Held on the 1st day of January, 2025 in the lobby.
Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Remote staff must complete training on the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Team leads should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Remote staff should review the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Remote staff may request changes to the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Team leads are encouraged to read the safety checklist through the internal portal. Employees should review the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Remote staff must complete training on the travel booking procedure within thirty days.

Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Managers should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Contractors are encouraged to read the holiday schedule within thirty days.
