# Remote Work Policy
Effective 12/9/2019

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 14 days per year, up to a cap of 39 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Team leads are encouraged to read the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Managers are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule through the internal portal.

Managers may request changes to the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Team leads should contact People Ops about the safety checklist within thirty days. Employees may request changes to the safety checklist within thirty days. Remote staff are encouraged to read the holiday schedule within thirty days. Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.

Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist within thirty days. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

New hires must complete training on the safety checklist during onboarding. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Remote staff may request changes to the holiday schedule during onboarding. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Contractors are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.
