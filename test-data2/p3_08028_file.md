# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective March 5, 2021

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 36 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Team leads should review the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. New hires should review the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.
