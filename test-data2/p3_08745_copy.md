# COMMON STOCK SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 2019-02-15 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $5,511.34, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

6. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

7. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.

### Additional Provisions

The disclosing party shall maintain accurate records of any inquiry from a governmental authority in accordance with the notice provisions. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any breach of the confidentiality obligations except as otherwise provided herein. Each party may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein.
