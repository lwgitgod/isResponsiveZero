# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective January 18, 2019

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 10 days per year, up to a cap of 29 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Contractors may request changes to the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Contractors should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Employees should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure through the internal portal.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
