# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 1st day of June, 2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 60% and Co-Founder 40% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $87,887.24 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

6. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

7. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

8. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

Any successor shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors as soon as reasonably practicable. The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. The recipient may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions. The undersigned shall not be liable for losses arising from its gross negligence upon written request. The parties waive trial by jury to the fullest extent permitted by law.

The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any successor shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section during the term and for one year thereafter. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. The Company shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations in accordance with the notice provisions.

The service provider shall promptly notify the other party of delays caused by third-party vendors as soon as reasonably practicable. No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. Either party shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations within ten (10) business days.

Any successor may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder as soon as reasonably practicable. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. Performance shall be rendered in a professional and workmanlike manner.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
