# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 2019-06-13  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Grace Haddad, counsel.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $69,348.93.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

Management reviewed the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Chair acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations without further action. The Board deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar with no objection. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting.
