**PAID TIME OFF POLICY**
Effective November 23, 2023

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 39 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

New hires must complete training on the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Managers must complete training on the safety checklist at least annually. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Remote staff are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle.

Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. New hires are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Contractors must complete training on the home-office stipend rules during onboarding.

Contractors must complete training on the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Managers are encouraged to read the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Managers should review the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window within thirty days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
