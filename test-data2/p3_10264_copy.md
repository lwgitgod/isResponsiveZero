# BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
(a Delaware corporation)

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Austin, TX or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each April at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 10% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be not less than one nor more than five. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.
