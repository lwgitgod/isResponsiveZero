# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 2019-06-19  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Derek Nguyen, counsel.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $67,807.93.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 2,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

### Housekeeping

Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. Management acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Chair acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines with no objection.

The Chair discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting and requested written materials in advance. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns.

It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. Management noted the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. Management discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary and directed follow-up by the officers. The Treasurer requested an update on the annual compliance calendar for information only.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
