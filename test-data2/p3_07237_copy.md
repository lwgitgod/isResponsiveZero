# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 1/15/2019  Place: offices of Marlow Pierce Stanton LLP
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Priya Dubois, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $27,357.44.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Summit Trust.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

The Treasurer reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Chair reviewed the draft budget template and requested written materials in advance. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The committee acknowledged receipt of the schedule for the next regular meeting and requested written materials in advance.

The committee discussed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management acknowledged receipt of the draft budget template and requested written materials in advance. The Treasurer noted the proposed meeting calendar for information only.

The Treasurer requested an update on the annual compliance calendar for information only. The committee deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board acknowledged receipt of the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action. The Chair acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The Secretary reviewed renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed.

No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action.

The committee acknowledged receipt of the annual compliance calendar and requested written materials in advance. The Board requested an update on the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
