# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: May 17, 2019  Place: offices of Delacroix Webb PC
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Victor Tanaka, counsel.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $63,839.78.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Pinecrest National Bank.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. The Secretary deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Board reviewed the draft budget template and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management discussed the proposed meeting calendar for information only.
