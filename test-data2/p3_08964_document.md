Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

### Procedures

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Team leads should review the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the safety checklist through the internal portal. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. New hires should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. New hires should review the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

New hires are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Team leads should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Managers are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. New hires must complete training on the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Remote staff may request changes to the safety checklist at least annually.

Team leads are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Managers may request changes to the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Team leads are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable.
