# Friends of the Eastside Library — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: 8/14/2020  Present: Chris Park (Chair), Farah Singh (Secretary), Helen Park, Owen Nguyen

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $3,833.35; the board voted to require two signatures on all checks.
3. Landscaping bids.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 5-0.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

### Housekeeping

The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Board requested an update on the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed.

The Chair deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action. It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually.

The Board discussed renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance. The Board reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines for information only. Management noted the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. Management acknowledged receipt of the annual compliance calendar and requested written materials in advance. The Secretary requested an update on the insurance broker's renewal summary with no objection.

Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. Management reviewed renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Board reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The committee deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy and requested written materials in advance.
