## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being all of the directors then in office of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective 2024-03-10:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 25,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $407.13.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-3 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "breach of founder obligations".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.
