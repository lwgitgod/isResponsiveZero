# Remote Work Policy
Effective March 4, 2022

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 21 days per year, up to a cap of 32 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
