## COMMON STOCK SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 9/2/2020 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $3,650.83, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

5. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
