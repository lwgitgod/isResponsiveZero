# Independent Appraisal of Common Stock Fair Market Value
Subject company: Brightline Ventures, Inc.   Valuation date: 9/25/2020
Prepared by: Carrow Valuation Advisors

1. Engagement. Hiring plans for the next four quarters include engineering, customer success and one finance generalist.
2. Methodology. We considered the income, market and asset approaches and relied primarily on the OPM method.
3. Discount for lack of marketability: 40%.
4. Conclusion. The fair market value of one share of Common Stock is $0.11.

Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.

Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.

Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.
