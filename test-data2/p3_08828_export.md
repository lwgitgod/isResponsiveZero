## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

Effective 4/27/2020, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline platform, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

The Company shall indemnify the other party against any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law. The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve the matters described in this section to the extent permitted by applicable law. Either party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter.

Each party shall maintain accurate records of the matters described in this section to the extent permitted by applicable law. The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. The Company shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section as soon as reasonably practicable. The Company shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. Any successor shall maintain accurate records of losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days.

The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions. Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail. The Company shall not be liable for any claim arising hereunder to the extent permitted by applicable law. The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any breach of the confidentiality obligations as soon as reasonably practicable.
