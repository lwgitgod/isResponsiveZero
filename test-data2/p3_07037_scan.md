# FOUNDERS' AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 1/17/2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $68,101.54 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

6. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

7. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

8. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

The service provider shall indemnify the other party against losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions. The Company shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions. The Company shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances upon written request. Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
