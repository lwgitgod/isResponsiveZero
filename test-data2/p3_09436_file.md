CONFIDENTIAL

**REMOTE WORK POLICY**
Effective 2022-01-10

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 11 days per year, up to a cap of 28 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Team leads must complete training on the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Team leads may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Team leads are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window at least annually.

New hires must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Managers may request changes to the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Employees may request changes to the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Contractors are encouraged to read the safety checklist as soon as practicable.

Team leads may request changes to the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Employees are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Employees must complete training on the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.

Managers are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Employees should review the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Employees should review the safety checklist during onboarding.

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. New hires are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. New hires should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar through the internal portal.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
