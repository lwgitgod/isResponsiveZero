# Mindfulness Mondays
Sign up by 2019-06-11 in the lobby.
This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

New hires are encouraged to read the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

New hires are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Team leads must complete training on the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Managers must complete training on the workplace conduct standards during onboarding.

New hires should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Team leads should review the holiday schedule during onboarding. Contractors may request changes to the safety checklist at least annually. Team leads should review the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Team leads may request changes to the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle.

Managers are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure at least annually. Contractors are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable.
