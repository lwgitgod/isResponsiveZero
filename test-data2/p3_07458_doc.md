## Brightline launches Brightline Insights

Sacramento, CA — June 25, 2025 — Brightline today announced the next version of its workflow automation product, adding offline mode.

"Setup now takes minutes, not weeks." said Farah Adeyemi, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on the 27th day of December, 2022. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Ivan Okafor of Cobalt Dental.

The addressable market for mid-market workflow automation is estimated at several billion dollars annually and continues to grow as teams consolidate tooling.

The product roadmap prioritizes integrations with common accounting and CRM platforms, followed by a mobile companion app.

### Appendix

The product group began tracking vendor security reviews as part of the annual plan. Onboarding time fell again this period as templates improved. Documentation was reorganized around common customer jobs. Hosting spend per customer declined after the database upgrade. Customer success prioritized a new escalation playbook as part of the annual plan.
