# Mindfulness Mondays
Held on August 13, 2022 in the break room.
Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

### Procedures

New hires are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Employees must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Employees must complete training on the safety checklist at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. New hires should review the holiday schedule during onboarding.

Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers should review the safety checklist within thirty days. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request.

Remote staff should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Managers may request changes to the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Contractors are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure at least annually.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Remote staff must complete training on the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Employees may request changes to the benefits enrollment window within thirty days.
