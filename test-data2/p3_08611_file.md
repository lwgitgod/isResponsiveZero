# FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of the 13th day of July, 2021 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 6,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $2,938.06, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares are fully vested upon issuance.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

5. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

6. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
