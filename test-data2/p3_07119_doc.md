# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective June 12, 2021

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 10 days per year, up to a cap of 21 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Procedures

Employees should review the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule within thirty days. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

Managers must complete training on the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Contractors may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure during onboarding.

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers must complete training on the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. New hires may request changes to the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Managers should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards during onboarding.

Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Managers are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.
