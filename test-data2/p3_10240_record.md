# Brightline — Company Overview 2023

**Why now** — Hiring plans for the next four quarters include engineering, customer success and one finance generalist.

**Product** — Drag-and-drop workflow builder, 44 integrations, SOC 2 Type I.

**Customers** — Mesa Property Group, Pinecrest Clinics, Cobalt Dental.

**Market** — Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.

The addressable market for mid-market workflow automation is estimated at several billion dollars annually and continues to grow as teams consolidate tooling.

**Competition** — Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.

**Financials** — Revenue 2021: $1018K; burn $145K/mo.
