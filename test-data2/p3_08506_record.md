# 409A Valuation Report
Subject company: Brightline Ventures, Inc.   Valuation date: March 10, 2022
Prepared by: Ledgerpoint Appraisal Group

1. Engagement. Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.
2. Methodology. We considered the income, market and asset approaches and relied primarily on the backsolve method.
3. Discount for lack of marketability: 25%.
4. Conclusion. The fair market value of one share of Common Stock is $0.02.

The addressable market for mid-market workflow automation is estimated at several billion dollars annually and continues to grow as teams consolidate tooling.

Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.
