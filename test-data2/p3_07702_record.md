## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Between Brightline Ventures, Inc. and Ridgeway Freight (each a "Party"), effective the 20th day of August, 2023.

Purpose: evaluation of a potential software subscription.

1. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

2. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

3. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

4. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

Brightline Ventures, Inc.: Lee Chen, Head of Partnerships
Counterparty: Derek Walsh, Procurement Manager

### Additional Provisions

The Company shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section upon written request. Any successor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter. The recipient shall promptly notify the other party of any claim arising hereunder within ten (10) business days. The disclosing party shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations as soon as reasonably practicable. Either party shall maintain accurate records of any material change in circumstances upon written request.

Nothing herein shall be construed to create a partnership or joint venture. The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference. Each party shall cooperate in good faith regarding losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. The Company shall maintain accurate records of any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions.

Either party shall cooperate in good faith regarding delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. The undersigned may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules except as otherwise provided herein. The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inaccuracy in the schedules within ten (10) business days.
