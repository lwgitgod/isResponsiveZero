### Background

The operations team streamlined renewal forecasting for mid-market accounts with encouraging early results. Net promoter score improved modestly quarter over quarter. Engineering prioritized vendor security reviews ahead of schedule.

Engineering reduced median page load time by roughly a third. The team completed migration of legacy reports to the new dashboard. The operations team began tracking vendor security reviews with encouraging early results. Finance published a new escalation playbook ahead of schedule.

Security questionnaires are now answered from a shared trust portal. Marketing completed a new escalation playbook in collaboration with two design partners. The operations team published the regional pricing experiment after customer feedback.
