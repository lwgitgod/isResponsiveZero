## FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of the 12th day of July, 2021 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $3,597.52, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares vest in equal monthly installments over 48 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### Additional Provisions

The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority within ten (10) business days. The undersigned shall indemnify the other party against the matters described in this section upon written request.
