# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective the 13th day of June, 2020

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 14 days per year, up to a cap of 39 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers may request changes to the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. New hires should review the holiday schedule at least annually. Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding.

Managers should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Remote staff should review the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure at least annually.

Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure within thirty days. New hires may request changes to the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Contractors should review the holiday schedule at least annually. Team leads are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure within thirty days.

Employees may request changes to the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Team leads are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable.

Employees should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. New hires are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. New hires should review the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Contractors should review the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Managers may request changes to the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Remote staff should review the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.
