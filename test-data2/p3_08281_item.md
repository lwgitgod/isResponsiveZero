# Independent Appraisal of Common Stock Fair Market Value
Subject company: Brightline Ventures, Inc.   Valuation date: 2020-03-20
Prepared by: Ledgerpoint Appraisal Group

1. Engagement. Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.
2. Methodology. We considered the income, market and asset approaches and relied primarily on the OPM method.
3. Discount for lack of marketability: 28%.
4. Conclusion. The fair market value of one share of Common Stock is $0.07.

The addressable market for mid-market workflow automation is estimated at several billion dollars annually and continues to grow as teams consolidate tooling.

Customer retention remained strong through the period, with most accounts expanding seats at renewal.
