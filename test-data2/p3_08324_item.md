# Remote Work Policy
Effective February 28, 2020

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 22 days per year, up to a cap of 25 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Team leads should review the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Employees should review the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.

New hires should review the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Managers are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Team leads should review the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Managers are required to acknowledge the safety checklist at least annually.

Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the safety checklist within thirty days. Remote staff are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. New hires should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Remote staff must complete training on the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Remote staff are encouraged to read the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. New hires should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.
