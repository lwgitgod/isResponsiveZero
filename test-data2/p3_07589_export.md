# Brightline Community Arts Council — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: 9/1/2019  Present: Leila Kim (Chair), Tomás Lindqvist (Secretary), Grace Patel, Chris Brennan

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $4,684.93; the board voted to require two signatures on all checks.
3. Spring fundraiser planning.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 6-0.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

### Housekeeping

The Board deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. Management acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations without further action. The committee deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy without further action.

The Secretary discussed the proposed meeting calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary deferred consideration of the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The Board reviewed the draft budget template and requested written materials in advance.
