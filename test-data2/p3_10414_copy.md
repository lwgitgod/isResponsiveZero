Held on March 26, 2023 in the break room.
Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

**PROCEDURES**

Team leads should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule during onboarding. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Managers are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Team leads should review the updated leave calendar at least annually. Team leads should review the travel booking procedure at least annually.

Team leads are required to acknowledge the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Employees are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule at least annually.

Employees should contact People Ops about the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Contractors should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Contractors should review the safety checklist through the internal portal.
