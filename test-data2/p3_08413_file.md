# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2019-03-05

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 20 days per year, up to a cap of 23 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Procedures

Employees should review the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Team leads may request changes to the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

Contractors are encouraged to read the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the safety checklist through the internal portal.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule during onboarding. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Team leads are encouraged to read the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

Unused balances are handled according to state law. Team leads should review the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Remote staff must complete training on the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal.

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure at least annually. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Team leads are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Team leads should review the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. New hires are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.

Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure during onboarding. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. New hires are encouraged to read the holiday schedule during onboarding.

Employees are required to acknowledge the safety checklist through the internal portal. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Team leads should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule during onboarding.

New hires may request changes to the updated leave calendar at least annually. Managers are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Contractors are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Remote staff must complete training on the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
