## Brightline Community Arts Council — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: the 26th day of November, 2025  Present: Mei Nguyen (Chair), Nina Brennan (Secretary), Tomás Moreno, Sam Moreno

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $4,118.51; the former treasurer has been asked to return the checkbook.
3. Field maintenance schedule.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 7-0.

The Board discussed the Corporation's fiscal year, which shall end on December 31 of each year unless otherwise determined by the Board.

### Other Business

The Chair discussed renewal of the general liability policy with no objection. Management deferred consideration of the draft budget template without further action. The Board requested an update on the updated records-retention guidelines for information only.

The Secretary discussed the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary reviewed the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.
