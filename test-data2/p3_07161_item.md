# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 1st day of July, 2020  Place: offices of Delacroix Webb PC
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Aisha Haddad, counsel.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $76,011.86.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Summit Trust.

The Chair reviewed the agenda and invited questions. There being none, the meeting proceeded to the business at hand.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

### Housekeeping

The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. The committee discussed renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Treasurer reviewed the annual compliance calendar without further action.

The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Chair noted the insurance broker's renewal summary for information only. The Treasurer requested an update on the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action.

Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The Chair noted the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management discussed the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Secretary noted the proposed meeting calendar for information only.

Management noted the annual compliance calendar and requested written materials in advance. The Board discussed the draft budget template without further action. The Chair reviewed renewal of the general liability policy with no objection.

The Board noted the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines without further action. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance.

The Board deferred consideration of the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The Treasurer reviewed the draft budget template without further action. Management deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

Management discussed the updated records-retention guidelines and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board. Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved.

The committee noted the annual compliance calendar and requested written materials in advance. It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The committee acknowledged receipt of the draft budget template with no objection.
