# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made 2023-11-14 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 655 Summit Dr, Austin, TX.

1. Services. Provider shall provide general management services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $13,410.76, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Twelve months, renewing automatically.

4. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

5. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

### General Terms

The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request. The service provider shall maintain accurate records of any inaccuracy in the schedules in accordance with the notice provisions. The disclosing party shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any claim arising hereunder except as otherwise provided herein. The recipient shall maintain accurate records of any claim arising hereunder within ten (10) business days.

Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive. The Company shall provide reasonable documentation of the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days. Either party shall provide reasonable documentation of any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law.
