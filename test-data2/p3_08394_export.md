# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 2019-06-11  Place: Austin, TX
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Omar Tanaka, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 5,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $43,958.48.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Harborview Savings.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board requested an update on the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Treasurer discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Secretary noted the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Board deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations with no objection. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
