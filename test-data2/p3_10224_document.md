## FOUNDERS' AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 2020-03-08, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

— Page 1 of 3 —

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $87,461.89 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

6. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### Additional Provisions

The undersigned shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any breach of the confidentiality obligations within ten (10) business days. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
