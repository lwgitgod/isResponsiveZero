# Flu Shot Clinic
Held on December 25, 2025 in Conference Room B.
Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Procedures

Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Remote staff may request changes to the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Remote staff may request changes to the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Contractors should review the travel booking procedure at least annually. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Team leads must complete training on the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Team leads must complete training on the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Managers are encouraged to read the holiday schedule during onboarding. Team leads are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure within thirty days.
