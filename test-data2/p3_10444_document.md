# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Between Brightline Ventures, Inc. and Mesa Property Group (each a "Party"), effective July 8, 2022.

Purpose: evaluation of a potential software subscription.

1. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

2. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

3. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

4. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

8. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

Brightline Ventures, Inc.: Owen Okafor, Head of Partnerships
Counterparty: Helen Schultz, CIO
