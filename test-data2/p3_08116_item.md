## Paid Time Off Policy
Effective March 13, 2023

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

— Page 1 of 2 —

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 17 days per year, up to a cap of 35 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

### Procedures

Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Employees should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Employees are required to acknowledge the safety checklist through the internal portal.

Team leads should contact People Ops about the safety checklist during onboarding. Managers are encouraged to read the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.

Team leads must complete training on the safety checklist within thirty days. Remote staff may request changes to the holiday schedule within thirty days. Contractors are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Remote staff must complete training on the travel booking procedure during onboarding.

Employees should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. New hires are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Employees should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Managers should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.
