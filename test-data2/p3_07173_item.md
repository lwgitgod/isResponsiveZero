# RESTRICTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of May 17, 2021 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $3,849.95, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares vest in equal monthly installments over 48 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

5. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

6. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

7. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
