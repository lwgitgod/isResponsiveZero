# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: the 18th day of September, 2020  Place: Austin, TX
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Paul Okafor, counsel.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $40,970.92.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 4,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Harborview Savings.

RESOLVED, that the form of corporate seal presented to the meeting is adopted as the seal of the Corporation.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.
