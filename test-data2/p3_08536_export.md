# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 1/4/2020  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Mei Brennan, counsel.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 6,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $63,317.20.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 2,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Meridian Commerce Bank.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

The Chair requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar without further action. The Secretary requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Discussion followed regarding the timing of the next regular meeting.

The Board noted the proposed meeting calendar and requested written materials in advance. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The Board noted the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary requested an update on the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance.

The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Secretary requested an update on the draft budget template and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board discussed the schedule for the next regular meeting without further action. The committee reviewed the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. Management discussed the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers.

It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The committee deferred consideration of renewal of the general liability policy for information only. The Board discussed the status of state tax registrations for information only. The committee acknowledged receipt of the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Chair requested an update on the annual compliance calendar for information only. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. Management discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. Management acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The Treasurer reviewed the annual compliance calendar without further action.

The committee discussed the updated records-retention guidelines for information only. The Secretary deferred consideration of the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board discussed the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary requested an update on the schedule for the next regular meeting and agreed to revisit it next quarter.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary
