# Parental Leave Policy
Effective 6/14/2021

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 16 days per year, up to a cap of 36 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

### Procedures

Contractors are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure at least annually. Employees should review the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires should review the safety checklist as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Remote staff should review the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Managers are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Team leads are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar through the internal portal.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.
