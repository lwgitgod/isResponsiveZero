# Flu Shot Clinic
Held on 2019-04-14 in the lobby.
Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

### Additional Guidance

Team leads are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Contractors must complete training on the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Employees should contact People Ops about the safety checklist during onboarding. Managers are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Employees must complete training on the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. New hires must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Team leads may request changes to the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule at least annually.
