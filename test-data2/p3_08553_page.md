# Parental Leave Policy
Effective February 23, 2021

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 23 days per year, up to a cap of 35 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule during onboarding. Team leads should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. New hires are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Team leads are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle.

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Team leads are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Team leads should review the safety checklist before their next review cycle.

Contractors are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Managers may request changes to the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Employees should contact People Ops about the safety checklist through the internal portal. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Employees may request changes to the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle.
