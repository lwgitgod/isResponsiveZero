# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: 7/7/2019  Place: offices of Kessler Vance LLP
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Dana Brennan, counsel.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $33,091.52.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at Summit Trust.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

### Housekeeping

The committee discussed the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. The officers were directed to maintain the corporate records at the principal office. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice.

The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The Chair deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The committee acknowledged receipt of the annual compliance calendar without further action.

The committee deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board noted the status of state tax registrations with no objection. The Secretary noted the schedule for the next regular meeting and requested written materials in advance.

The Chair acknowledged receipt of the draft budget template with no objection. The committee reviewed the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines with no objection. The Secretary reviewed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board discussed the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers.
