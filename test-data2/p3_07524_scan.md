### Frequently Asked Questions

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Managers are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Contractors are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards at least annually.

Contractors are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Managers may request changes to the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Managers are encouraged to read the holiday schedule within thirty days.

Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Team leads should contact People Ops about the safety checklist through the internal portal. Team leads are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure at least annually.

Remote staff should review the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Team leads should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
