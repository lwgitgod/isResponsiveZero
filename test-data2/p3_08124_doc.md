# Model Stockholders Agreement

This Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] among [COMPANY NAME], a [STATE] corporation (the "Company"), and [FOUNDER 1] and [FOUNDER 2] (each a "Shareholder").

1. Ownership. The Shareholders hold the following percentages: [FOUNDER 1] ___%; [FOUNDER 2] ___%.
2. Transfer Restrictions. No Shareholder may transfer shares without first offering them to the Company.
3. Drag-Along. Holders of ___% may require all Shareholders to join a sale.
4. Vesting. Shares vest over ___ months with a ___-month cliff.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

7. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

8. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

### Miscellaneous

All payments shall be made in United States dollars. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve the matters described in this section except as otherwise provided herein. The disclosing party shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations in accordance with the notice provisions. The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter.

The undersigned shall indemnify the other party against losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions. The Company shall provide reasonable documentation of the matters described in this section during the term and for one year thereafter. The disclosing party shall promptly notify the other party of delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. Either party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inaccuracy in the schedules within ten (10) business days.

Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void. Either party shall indemnify the other party against any claim arising hereunder upon written request. The parties waive trial by jury to the fullest extent permitted by law. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder.
