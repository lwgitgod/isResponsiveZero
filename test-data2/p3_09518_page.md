# Parental Leave Policy
Effective 2024-01-10

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 22 days per year, up to a cap of 22 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
