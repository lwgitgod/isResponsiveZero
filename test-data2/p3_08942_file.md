# Remote Work Policy
Effective 2022-11-14

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 33 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Contractors should review the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Managers should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule during onboarding. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Team leads should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar during onboarding.

Contractors may request changes to the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule during onboarding. Remote staff must complete training on the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.
