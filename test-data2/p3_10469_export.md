# FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of September 14, 2020 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,000,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $655.18, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

5. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.

Each party shall provide reasonable documentation of any claim arising hereunder except as otherwise provided herein. No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. Each party shall provide reasonable documentation of any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule. All payments shall be made in United States dollars. The Company shall promptly notify the other party of losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions. The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. Either party shall maintain accurate records of any claim arising hereunder as soon as reasonably practicable.
