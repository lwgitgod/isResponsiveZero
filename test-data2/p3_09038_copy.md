## Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2/3/2019

Purpose. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 15 days per year, up to a cap of 20 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Procedures

Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Contractors may request changes to the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the safety checklist before their next review cycle. New hires must complete training on the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins.

Managers are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Team leads must complete training on the safety checklist as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Contractors should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Employees are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Team leads are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Remote staff should review the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.

New hires must complete training on the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Employees are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule through the internal portal. New hires should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle.

Contractors should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Employees should review the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable.

New hires are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Managers should review the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Remote staff must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Managers should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.
