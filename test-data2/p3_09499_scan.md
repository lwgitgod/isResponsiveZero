# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being the sole director of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective the 17th day of November, 2023:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 25,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,945.72.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-4 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "non-vesting".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.

The Board acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Board deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The Secretary deferred consideration of the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The Board requested an update on the status of state tax registrations for information only.

The Secretary noted renewal of the general liability policy with no objection. Management provided a brief operational update, which was received without action.
