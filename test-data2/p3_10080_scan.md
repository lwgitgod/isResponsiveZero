# Remote Work Policy
Effective April 15, 2024

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 34 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Contractors should review the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Employees should review the holiday schedule before their next review cycle.

Team leads must complete training on the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Managers should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Employees should review the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
