# Flu Shot Clinic
Held on the 11th day of September, 2023 in Zoom.
Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

### Additional Guidance

Remote staff may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Employees are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Employees are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window within thirty days.

Remote staff may request changes to the holiday schedule within thirty days. Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist during onboarding. Remote staff are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. New hires must complete training on the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. New hires must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Employees must complete training on the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

Managers are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Employees should review the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Contractors may request changes to the safety checklist at least annually. Managers are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.
