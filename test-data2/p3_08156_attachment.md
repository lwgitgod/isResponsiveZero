# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective January 5, 2019

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 14 days per year, up to a cap of 32 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

New hires should contact People Ops about the safety checklist through the internal portal. Managers are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. New hires must complete training on the travel booking procedure during onboarding.

New hires may request changes to the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Remote staff must complete training on the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Team leads should review the holiday schedule as soon as practicable.

Managers must complete training on the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Employees should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Contractors are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure within thirty days.

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the safety checklist through the internal portal. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Contractors are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Employees are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards during onboarding.

Remote staff must complete training on the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Managers may request changes to the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Remote staff may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
