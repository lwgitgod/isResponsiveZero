# Parental Leave Policy
Effective 7/15/2020

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 23 days per year, up to a cap of 20 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Procedures

Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.

New hires should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule within thirty days. Unused balances are handled according to state law.

Employees must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Employees are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Team leads are required to acknowledge the safety checklist before their next review cycle.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.

Managers should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. New hires may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. New hires are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Remote staff are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Contractors are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Contractors must complete training on the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Managers are encouraged to read the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Team leads should review the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Contractors must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle.

Employees are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Contractors should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding.

Contractors should review the updated leave calendar during onboarding. New hires are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Managers are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Team leads must complete training on the safety checklist as soon as practicable.

New hires should review the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Remote staff must complete training on the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Employees are encouraged to read the safety checklist within thirty days. Employees must complete training on the holiday schedule through the internal portal.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Employees should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. New hires are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Contractors should review the travel booking procedure within thirty days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
