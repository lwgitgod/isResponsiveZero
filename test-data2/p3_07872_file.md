### Frequently Asked Questions

New hires must complete training on the travel booking procedure at least annually. New hires are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Managers are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. New hires must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days.

New hires should review the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist during onboarding. Team leads may request changes to the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Managers should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually.

Managers are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Remote staff must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Employees are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Contractors may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.
