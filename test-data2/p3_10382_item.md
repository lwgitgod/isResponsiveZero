## RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 2024-11-09

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "strategic advisory services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $16,150.29, retroactive to 2/24/2022;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at First Coastal Bank without further Board approval.

There being no further business, upon motion duly made and seconded, the meeting was adjourned.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

The Secretary requested an update on renewal of the general liability policy and agreed to revisit it next quarter. No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The Treasurer deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations without further action. The Board deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines with no objection.

The committee discussed the annual compliance calendar without further action. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. The Treasurer reviewed the annual compliance calendar for information only. The Secretary reviewed the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection.

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. The Board noted the status of state tax registrations without further action. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book.

The committee acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations and requested written materials in advance. The Board deferred consideration of the annual compliance calendar with no objection. Management deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The Secretary deferred consideration of the schedule for the next regular meeting for information only. The Treasurer acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers.

The Board deferred consideration of the proposed meeting calendar for information only. The Secretary discussed the draft budget template for information only. Management acknowledged receipt of the draft budget template and agreed to revisit it next quarter. The committee noted the annual compliance calendar and directed follow-up by the officers. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake. Director Alex Rivera: not present; no notice record located.
