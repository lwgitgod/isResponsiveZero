# Remote Work Policy
Effective April 10, 2023

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 18 days per year, up to a cap of 34 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Managers may request changes to the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Employees must complete training on the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.

New hires should review the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. New hires should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins.
