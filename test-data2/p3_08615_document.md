# CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 10/2/2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 60% and Co-Founder 40% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $33,990.84 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

6. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

7. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

8. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

9. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

The disclosing party shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned shall maintain accurate records of the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days.

Any successor shall promptly notify the other party of any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any successor shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. Either party shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder.

The disclosing party shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument.

Each party shall promptly notify the other party of delays caused by third-party vendors except as otherwise provided herein. The service provider shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request.
