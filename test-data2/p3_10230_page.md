## Remote Work Policy
Effective 2022-10-20

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 25 days per year, up to a cap of 34 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Employees are required to acknowledge the safety checklist through the internal portal. Contractors may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.

Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Managers should review the updated leave calendar at least annually. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure at least annually. Team leads are encouraged to read the safety checklist during onboarding. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Unused balances are handled according to state law. New hires must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days.
