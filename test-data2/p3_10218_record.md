# Ergonomics Workshop
Held on 7/6/2019 in Conference Room B.
Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Remote staff must complete training on the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Employees should review the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days. Team leads are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards during onboarding.

Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Remote staff must complete training on the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Managers may request changes to the safety checklist before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Employees must complete training on the holiday schedule at least annually.

Team leads are required to acknowledge the safety checklist at least annually. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Contractors must complete training on the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. New hires may request changes to the safety checklist through the internal portal.
