# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 2024-04-03

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "management services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $22,986.11, retroactive to the 20th day of January, 2022;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at Harborview Savings without further Board approval.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

The committee deferred consideration of the insurance broker's renewal summary and requested written materials in advance. The Board noted the draft budget template with no objection. The Secretary requested an update on the insurance broker's renewal summary and agreed to revisit it next quarter. It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually.

The Treasurer requested an update on the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee acknowledged receipt of the schedule for the next regular meeting with no objection. The Treasurer requested an update on the proposed meeting calendar with no objection. The Board requested an update on the annual compliance calendar for information only. The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice.

The Secretary discussed the annual compliance calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter. Management requested an update on the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The Chair reviewed the status of state tax registrations and directed follow-up by the officers.

Director voting in favor: Jordan Blake.
