# Brightline — Company Overview 2022

**Why now** — Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.

**Product** — Drag-and-drop workflow builder, 158 integrations, SOC 2 Type II.

**Customers** — Ridgeway Freight, Pinecrest Clinics, Otter Creek Foods.

**Market** — Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.

**Competition** — Key risks include longer enterprise sales cycles and concentration in a small number of large customers.

**Financials** — Revenue 2023: $895K; burn $148K/mo.

**Team** — Jordan Blake, Founder & CEO (100%) · Leila Dubois, VP Sales · Helen Nguyen, Head of Product

**Roadmap** — Customer retention remained strong through the period, with most accounts expanding seats at renewal.
