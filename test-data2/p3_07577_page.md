# Remote Work Policy
Effective the 1st day of April, 2023

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 11 days per year, up to a cap of 38 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Additional Guidance

Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

New hires should review the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Contractors should review the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Team leads are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Managers must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Remote staff are encouraged to read the holiday schedule at least annually. Contractors are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable.

Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Contractors are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. New hires may request changes to the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Contractors are encouraged to read the safety checklist as soon as practicable.
