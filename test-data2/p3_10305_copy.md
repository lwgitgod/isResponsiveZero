# Parental Leave Policy
Effective 12/11/2023

Purpose. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 18 days per year, up to a cap of 37 days.
