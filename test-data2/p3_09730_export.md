# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 4th day of November, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 60% and Co-Founder 40% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $37,359.21 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

6. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

7. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

8. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake

All payments shall be made in United States dollars. Either party shall maintain accurate records of any inaccuracy in the schedules within ten (10) business days. The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. Nothing herein shall be construed to create a partnership or joint venture.

Any successor shall indemnify the other party against losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions. The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of the matters described in this section upon written request. Each party shall not be liable for losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable.
