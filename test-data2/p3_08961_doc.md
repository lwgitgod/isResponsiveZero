## FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated September 12, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $57,687.02 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

6. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

7. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake

### General Terms

The undersigned shall indemnify the other party against any breach of the confidentiality obligations in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party shall indemnify the other party against any inaccuracy in the schedules during the term and for one year thereafter. Each party shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority in accordance with the notice provisions. Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions. The Company shall not be liable for any material change in circumstances upon written request.
