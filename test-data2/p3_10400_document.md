# FOUNDERS' AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 3/10/2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $87,363.94 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

6. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

7. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

8. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

9. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

### Miscellaneous

Each party may suspend performance in the event of delays caused by third-party vendors within ten (10) business days. The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding the matters described in this section except as otherwise provided herein. Any successor may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. Either party shall promptly notify the other party of any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
