# ACTION BY WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

The undersigned, being the sole director of Brightline Ventures, Inc., acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopts the following resolutions effective the 1st day of August, 2023:

1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 20,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,897.40.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-1 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "non-vesting".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.
