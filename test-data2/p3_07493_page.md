# Parental Leave Policy
Effective 2019-09-01

Purpose. Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 18 days per year, up to a cap of 27 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff should review the holiday schedule within thirty days. New hires are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window at least annually.

Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Remote staff may request changes to the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Managers may request changes to the safety checklist within thirty days. Team leads should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Contractors should contact People Ops about the safety checklist at least annually. Team leads must complete training on the workplace conduct standards within thirty days.

Managers are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Team leads are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar at least annually.

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist through the internal portal. Managers must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding.

Contractors are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
