# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2025-04-02

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 22 days per year, up to a cap of 40 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Remote staff must complete training on the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Team leads should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Remote staff must complete training on the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. New hires are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Remote staff should review the safety checklist at least annually. Team leads should review the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Remote staff may request changes to the safety checklist at least annually.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.
