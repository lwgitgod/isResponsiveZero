# MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Date: November 11, 2020  Place: by videoconference
Present: Alex Rivera (Director), Jordan Blake (Director). Also present: Farah Okafor, counsel.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

Issuance of Founder Stock. RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 7,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Alex Rivera, as founder, in exchange for the assignment of the Brightline software platform and related intellectual property and cash of $39,611.21.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation issue 3,500,000 shares of Common Stock to Jordan Blake in consideration of services rendered in connection with the incorporation of the Corporation.

Election of Officers. RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is elected President and Jordan Blake is elected Secretary and Treasurer, with authority to open and sign on the Corporation's bank accounts at First Coastal Bank.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

/s/ Jordan Blake, Secretary

### Housekeeping

The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. The Secretary acknowledged receipt of the status of state tax registrations without further action. The Treasurer deferred consideration of the updated records-retention guidelines without further action.

Management deferred consideration of the status of state tax registrations for information only. Management requested an update on the updated records-retention guidelines and requested written materials in advance. Management requested an update on the status of state tax registrations and agreed to revisit it next quarter.
