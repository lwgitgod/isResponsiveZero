# Mindfulness Mondays
Held on 2021-10-15 in Conference Room B.
This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Contractors are required to acknowledge the safety checklist through the internal portal. Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Managers may request changes to the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Remote staff are encouraged to read the safety checklist at least annually. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Employees must complete training on the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards before their next review cycle. Managers must complete training on the safety checklist during onboarding.

New hires may request changes to the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Managers should review the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle.
