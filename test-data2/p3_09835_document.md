1. Issuance. The Corporation shall issue 20,000,000 shares of Common Stock to Blake Holdings LLC for consideration of $1,216.83.

2. Cancellation. Certificate CS-1 registered to Alex Rivera is hereby cancelled and returned to authorized but unissued status for "breach of founder obligations".

3. Ledger. The Secretary is directed to update the stock ledger to reflect the foregoing.
