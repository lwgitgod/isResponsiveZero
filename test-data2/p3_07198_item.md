# Remote Work Policy
Effective the 12th day of February, 2019

Purpose. Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 18 days per year, up to a cap of 25 days.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Team leads are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Managers are encouraged to read the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Contractors are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Employees should review the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. New hires may request changes to the holiday schedule through the internal portal. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.
