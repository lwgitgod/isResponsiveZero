## Remote Work Policy
Effective the 16th day of August, 2024

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 23 days per year, up to a cap of 35 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. New hires should review the holiday schedule at least annually. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. New hires should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines within thirty days.

Contractors are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Unused balances are handled according to state law. New hires may request changes to the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Managers must complete training on the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
