## RESTRICTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 6/26/2020 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,000,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $844.64, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Successors and Assigns. This instrument shall bind and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

5. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

6. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

7. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

8. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.
