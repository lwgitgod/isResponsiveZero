# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 12/10/2022

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 16 days per year, up to a cap of 34 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

### Procedures

New hires should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule within thirty days. New hires should contact People Ops about the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Team leads are required to acknowledge the safety checklist during onboarding. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually. Managers are encouraged to read the holiday schedule through the internal portal.

Unused balances are handled according to state law. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Employees are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Employees are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Contractors must complete training on the updated leave calendar during onboarding.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.
