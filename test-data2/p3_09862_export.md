**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Master Services Agreement is made 11/10/2023 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 354 Summit Dr, Raleigh, NC.

1. Services. Provider shall provide general management services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $13,304.79, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Three years.

4. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

5. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

6. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

7. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding the matters described in this section upon written request. The undersigned shall cooperate in good faith regarding the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days. The disclosing party shall promptly notify the other party of losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. Either party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any material change in circumstances within ten (10) business days.

The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions. Any successor shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority upon written request. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive.

Each party shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein. The recipient shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void.

The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference.
