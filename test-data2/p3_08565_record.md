### General Terms

Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. The service provider shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable. Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule. The undersigned shall maintain accurate records of any material change in circumstances in accordance with the notice provisions. The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees.

Either party shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein. Either party shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules except as otherwise provided herein. The disclosing party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. Each party represents that it has full power and authority to enter into this instrument. Performance shall be rendered in a professional and workmanlike manner.

No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. The Company shall cooperate in good faith regarding any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. The disclosing party shall promptly notify the other party of any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter.

The disclosing party shall provide reasonable documentation of any claim arising hereunder within ten (10) business days. The service provider shall promptly notify the other party of the matters described in this section upon written request. The Company shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. Either party shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. Any successor shall promptly notify the other party of any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein.
