# Paid Time Off Policy
Effective 2/18/2021

Purpose. Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 11 days per year, up to a cap of 29 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Managers are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Managers are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Managers are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the holiday schedule through the internal portal.

Team leads should review the safety checklist before their next review cycle. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Team leads must complete training on the travel booking procedure at least annually. Contractors are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar at least annually. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.

New hires may request changes to the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Team leads should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Team leads should review the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Employees are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable.
