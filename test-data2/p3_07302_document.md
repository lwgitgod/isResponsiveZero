# Parental Leave Policy
Effective 2020-02-22

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 23 days per year, up to a cap of 30 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

### Procedures

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Team leads may request changes to the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Employees should review the safety checklist during onboarding. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.

Managers should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Employees are encouraged to read the safety checklist before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure at least annually. New hires may request changes to the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. New hires are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable.

New hires should review the travel booking procedure during onboarding. New hires must complete training on the safety checklist through the internal portal. Team leads are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules during onboarding. Managers should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
