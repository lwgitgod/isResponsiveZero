# Remote Work Policy
Effective the 14th day of March, 2025

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 14 days per year, up to a cap of 29 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

### Procedures

Contractors should review the holiday schedule during onboarding. Team leads should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule during onboarding. Managers should review the updated leave calendar at least annually. Employees are required to acknowledge the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Employees may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable.

Contractors should review the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. New hires are required to acknowledge the safety checklist within thirty days. New hires should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards within thirty days.

New hires are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Employees are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. Team leads should contact People Ops about the safety checklist through the internal portal. Remote staff are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure within thirty days.

Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. New hires should review the expense reimbursement guidelines before their next review cycle. Team leads may request changes to the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Team leads are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle.

New hires should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Team leads must complete training on the safety checklist through the internal portal. Employees must complete training on the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle.

Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Employees should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Remote staff are encouraged to read the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Team leads should review the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Questions about this policy should be directed to your manager or to the People team.
