# Travel & Expense Policy
Effective April 2, 2024

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 14 days per year, up to a cap of 22 days.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Team leads may request changes to the benefits enrollment window at least annually. Employees are required to acknowledge the safety checklist within thirty days. Remote staff must complete training on the holiday schedule during onboarding. Employees are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request.

Managers are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar at least annually. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Team leads are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule at least annually. Unused balances are handled according to state law. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles.

Remote staff must complete training on the safety checklist through the internal portal. Remote staff should review the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Managers are encouraged to read the safety checklist during onboarding. New hires should review the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal.

New hires must complete training on the safety checklist during onboarding. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal. Team leads are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable.

Employees should review the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the safety checklist within thirty days. Employees must complete training on the benefits enrollment window within thirty days.

Remote staff should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the holiday schedule through the internal portal. Team leads should review the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Contractors are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable.

Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule at least annually. Team leads are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle. Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars.

Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Team leads are encouraged to read the safety checklist through the internal portal. Employees should contact People Ops about the safety checklist through the internal portal. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Remote staff should review the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Managers must complete training on the travel booking procedure at least annually. Contractors are encouraged to read the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Remote staff are encouraged to read the holiday schedule at least annually.

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule at least annually. Managers should respond to requests within three business days.

Team leads should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually. Employees should review the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Remote staff should review the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation. Contractors are required to acknowledge the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
