# TECHNOLOGY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective 2019-06-11, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline scheduling engine, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

Any successor shall provide reasonable documentation of losses arising from its gross negligence except as otherwise provided herein. The disclosing party may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter. The service provider may suspend performance in the event of any breach of the confidentiality obligations within ten (10) business days. The Company shall promptly notify the other party of losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law.
