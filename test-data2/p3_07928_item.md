**REMOTE WORK POLICY**
Effective 2025-09-13

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 30 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Managers are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Contractors should review the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Team leads are encouraged to read the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle.

Managers are required to acknowledge the updated leave calendar during onboarding. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Managers may request changes to the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis.

Remote staff are encouraged to read the safety checklist at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the benefits enrollment window during onboarding. Employees may request changes to the holiday schedule at least annually. Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal.

New hires must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Contractors should review the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Team leads are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
