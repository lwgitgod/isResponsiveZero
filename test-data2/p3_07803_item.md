# Friends of the Eastside Library — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: 4/8/2021  Present: Farah Russo (Chair), Sofia Kim (Secretary), Lee Schultz, Priya Walsh

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $1,376.02; the board voted to require two signatures on all checks.
3. Book sale volunteers.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 6-0.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to pay all fees and expenses incident to the organization of the Corporation.

### Housekeeping

The Secretary discussed the draft budget template for information only. It was noted that insurance coverage should be reviewed annually. The officers were authorized to engage accountants for the preparation of tax returns.

Management noted the updated records-retention guidelines and directed follow-up by the officers. The committee noted renewal of the general liability policy and directed follow-up by the officers. The Board acknowledged receipt of the proposed meeting calendar for information only.
