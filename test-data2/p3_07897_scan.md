## FOUNDERS' AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 2019-12-07, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 65% and Co-Founder 35% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $70,729.93 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

6. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.
