# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made 2021-02-04 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 318 Harbor Dr, Denver, CO.

1. Services. Provider shall provide back-office administration services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $20,644.24, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Until terminated by Provider.

4. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

5. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

6. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

7. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

8. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

CLIENT: Brightline Ventures, Inc., by Jordan Blake, Treasurer      PROVIDER: Blake Holdings LLC, by Jordan Blake, Manager

Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void. Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve delays caused by third-party vendors within ten (10) business days.

Each party shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inaccuracy in the schedules within ten (10) business days. Any successor shall not be liable for losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years. Either party shall provide reasonable documentation of delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees. Remedies provided herein are cumulative and not exclusive of any remedies provided by law. Any successor shall not be liable for any material change in circumstances upon written request. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent.
