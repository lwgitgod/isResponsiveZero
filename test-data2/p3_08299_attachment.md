## TECHNOLOGY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Effective 9/26/2021, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline platform, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").

Consideration. In consideration for the Technology, the Company shall issue to Assignor 6,500,000 shares of Common Stock as set forth in the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement.

Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

The recipient shall indemnify the other party against any breach of the confidentiality obligations in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law. The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any claim arising hereunder to the extent permitted by applicable law. Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule.

The disclosing party shall promptly notify the other party of any inquiry from a governmental authority within ten (10) business days. The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve the matters described in this section as soon as reasonably practicable. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void.

Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years. Any successor shall provide reasonable documentation of losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. Performance shall be rendered in a professional and workmanlike manner.
