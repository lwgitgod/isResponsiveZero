# CO-FOUNDER AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 2019-11-12, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 65% and Co-Founder 35% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $85,163.09 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder. Either party may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules except as otherwise provided herein. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. Records relating to performance shall be retained for not less than three years.

Nothing herein shall be construed to create a partnership or joint venture. Any successor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter. Each party shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request.

Any successor shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter. Any assignment in violation of this section shall be null and void. The singular includes the plural and vice versa, as the context requires. Any successor shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein.

Each party shall maintain accurate records of losses arising from its gross negligence except as otherwise provided herein. Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this instrument. The service provider shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors except as otherwise provided herein.

Each party may suspend performance in the event of any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein. Either party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions. Any successor shall maintain accurate records of the matters described in this section to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Any successor shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. The Company shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. Either party shall cooperate in good faith regarding delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions.

All payments shall be made in United States dollars. The recipient shall not be liable for any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. The disclosing party shall indemnify the other party against losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable.

Any successor shall maintain accurate records of any breach of the confidentiality obligations during the term and for one year thereafter. Remedies provided herein are cumulative and not exclusive of any remedies provided by law. Each party may suspend performance in the event of delays caused by third-party vendors except as otherwise provided herein. The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence upon written request. The undersigned shall provide reasonable documentation of any inquiry from a governmental authority except as otherwise provided herein.
