# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 5th day of February, 2020, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 60% and Co-Founder 40% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $88,529.45 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

6. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

7. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

8. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

Each party may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. The service provider shall promptly notify the other party of losses arising from its gross negligence within ten (10) business days. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. The service provider shall promptly notify the other party of any inaccuracy in the schedules upon written request.

The Company may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions. The recipient shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned shall promptly notify the other party of any material change in circumstances to the extent permitted by applicable law. Delivery of an executed signature page by electronic transmission shall be effective as delivery of an original. Either party shall not be liable for delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions.

The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. Capitalized terms used but not defined have the meanings given in the applicable schedule. The Company shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter. The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
