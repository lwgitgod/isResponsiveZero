# CLE Seminar Materials — Equity for Early-Stage Companies
Presenter: Tomás Schultz, Tidewater Law Partners   Credit: 3.0 hours

I. Choosing the entity: C-corp vs. LLC
II. The certificate of incorporation: required provisions under DGCL §102
III. Organizational actions: bylaws, initial board, officer elections
IV. Founder stock: purchase agreements, vesting, and the 30-day §83(b) deadline
V. Cap table hygiene and the stock ledger (DGCL §224)
VI. Common mistakes: incorporator never resigns; shares never actually issued

### Standard Terms and Conditions

The Company shall promptly notify the other party of the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days. Each party shall not be liable for any material change in circumstances during the term and for one year thereafter. Either party shall maintain accurate records of the matters described in this section upon written request. All payments shall be made in United States dollars.

The prevailing party in any action to enforce this instrument shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees. The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve the matters described in this section during the term and for one year thereafter. Time is of the essence with respect to every obligation hereunder.

Each party shall indemnify the other party against losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned shall not be liable for any inaccuracy in the schedules as soon as reasonably practicable. The Company may suspend performance in the event of any inaccuracy in the schedules except as otherwise provided herein. The disclosing party shall maintain accurate records of the matters described in this section during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law.
