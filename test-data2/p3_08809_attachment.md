# Ergonomics Workshop
Held on the 1st day of December, 2020 in the lobby.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Team leads should contact People Ops about the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the holiday schedule as soon as practicable. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.
