# BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted by the Board of Directors

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Denver, CO or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each October at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 10% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be three. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.

ARTICLE V — STOCK. Certificates shall be signed by the President and the Secretary. Transfers shall be made only on the stock ledger of the Corporation upon surrender of the certificate duly endorsed.

Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

CERTIFICATE OF SECRETARY
I, Jordan Blake, Secretary of Brightline Ventures, Inc., certify that the foregoing Bylaws were adopted by the Board of Directors on 11/20/2019.
