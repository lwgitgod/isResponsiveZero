# FOUNDERS' MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated 11/8/2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 70% and Co-Founder 30% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $59,049.73 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Confidentiality. Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information received from the other party and shall not disclose it except to advisors bound by similar obligations.

6. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

### Standard Terms and Conditions

All payments shall be made in United States dollars. The undersigned shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority within ten (10) business days. The disclosing party shall indemnify the other party against delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request. The singular includes the plural and vice versa, as the context requires.

Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. The disclosing party shall cooperate in good faith regarding any claim arising hereunder during the term and for one year thereafter. The undersigned shall not be liable for the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days. Any successor shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein. The undersigned shall cooperate in good faith regarding any breach of the confidentiality obligations upon written request.
