# Parental Leave Policy
Effective October 1, 2021

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 10 days per year, up to a cap of 39 days.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Employees may request changes to the updated leave calendar before their next review cycle. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Employees may request changes to the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Employees are encouraged to read the benefits enrollment window within thirty days.

New hires should review the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window through the internal portal. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. New hires are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days.

Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. New hires are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards as soon as practicable. Team leads are required to acknowledge the safety checklist within thirty days.

Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace. Managers should contact People Ops about the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Contractors should review the workplace conduct standards within thirty days. Employees are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Team leads are required to acknowledge the workplace conduct standards during onboarding.

Contractors may request changes to the safety checklist as soon as practicable. Contractors must complete training on the home-office stipend rules as soon as practicable. Contractors should review the safety checklist before their next review cycle. New hires are encouraged to read the workplace conduct standards within thirty days.

Team leads are encouraged to read the safety checklist during onboarding. Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. New hires may request changes to the safety checklist as soon as practicable. New hires may request changes to the safety checklist within thirty days. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins.

Team leads are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. Managers must complete training on the updated leave calendar as soon as practicable. New hires must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines at least annually.

Remote staff should review the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards at least annually.

Team leads should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules before their next review cycle.
