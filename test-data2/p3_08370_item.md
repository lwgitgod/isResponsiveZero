# Parental Leave Policy
Effective April 20, 2022

Purpose. This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 30 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 19 days per year, up to a cap of 39 days.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

Employees are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Unused balances are handled according to state law. New hires may request changes to the home-office stipend rules during onboarding.

Contractors should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Managers must complete training on the safety checklist at least annually. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. New hires are required to acknowledge the benefits enrollment window as soon as practicable.
