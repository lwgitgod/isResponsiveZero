# FOUNDERS' AGREEMENT
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Dated the 11th day of October, 2019, between Alex Rivera ("Founder") and Jordan Blake ("Co-Founder").

1. Ownership. Founder shall hold 75% and Co-Founder 25% of the issued Common Stock of the Company.
2. Contributions. Founder contributes the Brightline platform and $32,986.14 in seed capital. Co-Founder contributes incorporation, banking setup and administrative services.
3. Incorporation. Co-Founder shall incorporate the Company on Founder's behalf and shall hold no shares other than as provided in Section 1.
4. Decisions. Issuance of additional shares requires the written consent of both Founders.

5. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

6. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

7. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

8. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

9. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

10. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

Each party shall indemnify the other party against any claim arising hereunder upon written request. The disclosing party shall indemnify the other party against any inquiry from a governmental authority upon written request. The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference. The singular includes the plural and vice versa, as the context requires. The recipient may suspend performance in the event of delays caused by third-party vendors during the term and for one year thereafter.

Signed: Alex Rivera        Jordan Blake
