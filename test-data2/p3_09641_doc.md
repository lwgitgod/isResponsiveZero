# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made August 3, 2021 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 921 Lake Dr, Portland, OR.

1. Services. Provider shall provide strategic advisory services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $18,029.83, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Three years.

4. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

5. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

6. Independent Counsel. Each party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to consult independent legal counsel of its choosing before executing this instrument.

7. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

8. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

9. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

### Miscellaneous

Either party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any claim arising hereunder in accordance with the notice provisions. The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding delays caused by third-party vendors as soon as reasonably practicable. The Company may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter.
