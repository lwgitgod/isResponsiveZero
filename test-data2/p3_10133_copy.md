# Friends of the Eastside Library — Minutes of the Board Meeting
Date: the 24th day of July, 2025  Present: Owen Adeyemi (Chair), Mei Ferreira (Secretary), Sam Dubois, Priya Brennan

1. Minutes of prior meeting approved.
2. Treasurer's report: the reconciliation shows a shortfall of $1,799.14; the board voted to require two signatures on all checks.
3. Spring fundraiser planning.
4. Bylaw amendment to add a second signatory: approved 4-0.

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse reasonable organizational expenses incurred in connection with its formation.

### Additional Matters

The meeting was called to order at the time stated in the notice. The Secretary was directed to file a copy of these proceedings with the minute book. Signatories on deposit accounts shall be as designated from time to time by the Board.

The Board discussed the draft budget template and directed follow-up by the officers. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Board discussed the annual compliance calendar for information only. The Chair acknowledged receipt of renewal of the general liability policy for information only.

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the matter was approved. The Chair confirmed that all attendees could hear one another. The Board acknowledged receipt of the insurance broker's renewal summary without further action. Management requested an update on the annual compliance calendar for information only.

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.
