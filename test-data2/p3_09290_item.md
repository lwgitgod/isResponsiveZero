**BYLAWS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.**
As adopted 7/13/2020

ARTICLE I — OFFICES. The principal office shall be located in Madison, WI or such other place as the Board may designate.

ARTICLE II — STOCKHOLDERS. The annual meeting of stockholders shall be held each October at a time fixed by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the President or by holders of not less than 20% of the outstanding shares.

ARTICLE III — DIRECTORS. The number of directors shall be two. Directors shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast.

ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS. The officers shall be a President, a Secretary and a Treasurer. The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporate funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.

ARTICLE V — STOCK. Certificates shall be signed by the President and the Secretary. Transfers shall be made only on the stock ledger of the Corporation upon surrender of the certificate duly endorsed.

ARTICLE VI — INDEMNIFICATION. The Corporation shall indemnify its directors and officers to the fullest extent permitted by the General Corporation Law.



CERTIFICATE OF SECRETARY
I, Jordan Blake, Secretary of Brightline Ventures, Inc., certify that the foregoing Bylaws were adopted by the Board of Directors on 7/13/2020.
/s/ Jordan Blake
