# MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS
Brightline Ventures, Inc.

Effective the 24th day of May, 2024, the undersigned, representing a majority of the outstanding voting shares, hereby:

RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed as a director of the Corporation, effective immediately;
RESOLVED, that Alex Rivera is removed from the office of President, and Jordan Blake is appointed President and Chief Executive Officer;
RESOLVED, that the size of the Board is fixed at one (1).

The Board reviewed the requirement to obtain an employer identification number and to register to do business in the states where the Corporation operates.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all actions and execute all documents as may be necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions.

### Additional Matters

No objection was raised to the conduct of the meeting. The fiscal calendar and reporting cadence were reviewed and confirmed. The Treasurer reviewed the annual compliance calendar for information only. The Treasurer noted the proposed meeting calendar and agreed to revisit it next quarter.
