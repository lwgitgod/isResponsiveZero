# Remote Work Policy
Effective 2024-11-26

Purpose. Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 17 days per year, up to a cap of 31 days.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.

### Additional Guidance

Managers may request changes to the benefits enrollment window before their next review cycle. Managers should review the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Employees should record time off in the HR system before the absence begins. New hires must complete training on the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal.

The company reimburses approved expenses within two pay cycles. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday. Remote staff are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis. Remote staff may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding.

Employees may request changes to the travel booking procedure at least annually. New hires should review the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Training on this policy is part of new-hire orientation.

Remote staff are required to acknowledge the holiday schedule at least annually. Managers may request changes to the safety checklist before their next review cycle. New hires may request changes to the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Contractors are required to acknowledge the travel booking procedure before their next review cycle.

Remote staff should review the holiday schedule during onboarding. Employees should contact People Ops about the safety checklist through the internal portal. Team leads should review the holiday schedule at least annually. New hires should review the benefits enrollment window within thirty days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.
