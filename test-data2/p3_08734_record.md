This Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] among [COMPANY NAME], a [STATE] corporation (the "Company"), and [FOUNDER 1] and [FOUNDER 2] (each a "Shareholder").

1. Ownership. The Shareholders hold the following percentages: [FOUNDER 1] ___%; [FOUNDER 2] ___%.
2. Transfer Restrictions. No Shareholder may transfer shares without first offering them to the Company.
3. Drag-Along. Holders of ___% may require all Shareholders to join a sale.
4. Vesting. Shares vest over ___ months with a ___-month cliff.

5. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

6. Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, pandemic, war, labor disputes or governmental action.

7. Construction. The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to a statute include any amendment or successor thereto.

### Miscellaneous

The undersigned shall maintain accurate records of any inaccuracy in the schedules to the extent permitted by applicable law. The undersigned shall cooperate in good faith regarding losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail.

Each party shall promptly notify the other party of any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein. Either party shall not be liable for any inquiry from a governmental authority as soon as reasonably practicable. The disclosing party shall maintain accurate records of any inquiry from a governmental authority upon written request.

The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence during the term and for one year thereafter. The disclosing party shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. The recipient shall not be liable for any claim arising hereunder upon written request. Either party shall not be liable for any material change in circumstances except as otherwise provided herein.

The recipient shall maintain accurate records of any inquiry from a governmental authority to the extent permitted by applicable law. The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. The disclosing party shall not be liable for the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions.

The service provider shall cooperate in good faith regarding any inaccuracy in the schedules except as otherwise provided herein. The recipient shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request. The recipient shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors as soon as reasonably practicable. Each party shall cooperate in good faith regarding the matters described in this section within ten (10) business days. Each party may suspend performance in the event of any claim arising hereunder as soon as reasonably practicable.

The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any breach of the confidentiality obligations as soon as reasonably practicable. The Company shall maintain accurate records of delays caused by third-party vendors to the extent permitted by applicable law. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive. The Company shall provide reasonable documentation of any material change in circumstances within ten (10) business days. The disclosing party may suspend performance in the event of any inquiry from a governmental authority during the term and for one year thereafter.
