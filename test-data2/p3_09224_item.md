# RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BRIGHTLINE VENTURES, INC.
Adopted 1/28/2024

WHEREAS, Blake Holdings LLC, an entity controlled by Jordan Blake, has provided "operational consulting services" to the Corporation;

RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall pay Blake Holdings LLC a monthly fee of $19,568.08, retroactive to November 25, 2021;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that Jordan Blake, as Treasurer, is authorized to effect such payments by wire from the Corporation's operating account at First Coastal Bank without further Board approval.

The Secretary noted that notice of the meeting had been duly given or waived and that a quorum was present throughout.
