Effective the 22nd day of May, 2020, Alex Rivera ("Assignor"), a founder of Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company"), hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Brightline scheduling engine, including source code repositories, trademarks, domain names (brightlineventures.com) and all related know-how (the "Technology").
