# Remote Work Policy
Effective 2022-09-22

Purpose. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Eligibility. All full-time employees are eligible after 60 days of employment.

Accrual. Employees accrue 12 days per year, up to a cap of 26 days.

Nothing in this policy alters the at-will nature of employment where applicable law permits.

Managers are responsible for ensuring coverage during approved absences.

Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and to act consistently with the company's code of conduct.

Employees are encouraged to read the holiday schedule through the internal portal. New hires are required to acknowledge the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal. New hires must complete training on the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Remote employees must maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace.

Team leads are encouraged to read the holiday schedule within thirty days. Employees must complete training on the updated leave calendar at least annually. New hires are encouraged to read the expense reimbursement guidelines during onboarding. Contractors may request changes to the workplace conduct standards at least annually. Remote staff may request changes to the safety checklist before their next review cycle.

Managers may request changes to the expense reimbursement guidelines through the internal portal. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the safety checklist at least annually. Team leads may request changes to the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Team leads may request changes to the workplace conduct standards during onboarding.

Receipts are required for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Managers are encouraged to read the travel booking procedure through the internal portal. Remote staff may request changes to the travel booking procedure as soon as practicable. Business travel should be booked through the approved portal.

Reasonable accommodations are available upon request. Remote staff must complete training on the workplace conduct standards through the internal portal. Managers may request changes to the workplace conduct standards during onboarding. Managers must complete training on the safety checklist before their next review cycle. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Remote staff may request changes to the safety checklist through the internal portal. Contractors should review the holiday schedule before their next review cycle. Remote staff may request changes to the workplace conduct standards during onboarding.

Managers must complete training on the expense reimbursement guidelines as soon as practicable. Contractors should review the home-office stipend rules within thirty days. Employees should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure during onboarding. Managers should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window during onboarding.

Team leads are required to acknowledge the safety checklist within thirty days. Managers should respond to requests within three business days. Contractors should contact People Ops about the updated leave calendar through the internal portal. Holidays falling on a weekend are observed on the nearest weekday.

New hires should contact People Ops about the benefits enrollment window within thirty days. Remote staff should contact People Ops about the travel booking procedure within thirty days. Team leads should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule at least annually. Team leads are encouraged to read the home-office stipend rules through the internal portal.

Contractors should review the updated leave calendar within thirty days. Employees should contact People Ops about the holiday schedule within thirty days. Unused balances are handled according to state law. Team leads should contact People Ops about the home-office stipend rules at least annually.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on the internal wiki.
