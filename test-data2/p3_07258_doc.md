# FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of 2/28/2019 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 7,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $6,675.59, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. 25% of the shares vest on the first anniversary, with the balance vesting monthly over 36 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

5. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

6. Entire Agreement. This instrument, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding of the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and agreements.

7. Governing Law. This instrument shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

8. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
