# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made the 13th day of March, 2021 between Brightline Ventures, Inc. ("Client") and Blake Holdings LLC ("Provider"), 991 Harbor Dr, Austin, TX.

1. Services. Provider shall provide general management services as requested by Client.
2. Fees. Client shall pay Provider a fixed monthly retainer of $9,667.74, payable in advance by wire.
3. Term. Twelve months, renewing automatically.

4. Notices. All notices shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered personally, one business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or upon confirmed transmission by email.

5. Headings. Section headings are for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of this instrument.

6. Amendment and Waiver. No amendment or waiver of any provision shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. No failure to exercise any right shall operate as a waiver thereof.

7. Dispute Resolution. Any controversy arising under this instrument shall first be submitted to good-faith negotiation between senior representatives for a period of thirty (30) days before any party commences proceedings.

8. Severability. If any provision of this instrument is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be reformed to the minimum extent necessary.

9. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

10. Counterparts. This instrument may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signatures shall be deemed originals.

### General Terms

Nothing herein shall be construed to create a partnership or joint venture. Any notice of default shall describe the default in reasonable detail. The recitals set forth above are incorporated herein by reference. The undersigned shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve losses arising from its gross negligence in accordance with the notice provisions.

The service provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve delays caused by third-party vendors upon written request. Neither party shall make any public announcement regarding this instrument without prior consent. Obligations that by their nature should survive termination shall so survive.

No third party is intended to be a beneficiary of this instrument. The exhibits and schedules form an integral part of this instrument. Any successor shall cooperate in good faith regarding the matters described in this section in accordance with the notice provisions. Either party shall not be liable for any breach of the confidentiality obligations to the extent permitted by applicable law. The Company may suspend performance in the event of losses arising from its gross negligence to the extent permitted by applicable law.

The recipient shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve delays caused by third-party vendors in accordance with the notice provisions. The Company shall promptly notify the other party of losses arising from its gross negligence as soon as reasonably practicable. Either party shall indemnify the other party against any material change in circumstances as soon as reasonably practicable.
