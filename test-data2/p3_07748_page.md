# Independent Appraisal of Common Stock Fair Market Value
Subject company: Brightline Ventures, Inc.   Valuation date: 3/21/2021
Prepared by: Summit Fair Value LLC

1. Engagement. Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.
2. Methodology. We considered the income, market and asset approaches and relied primarily on the PWERM method.
3. Discount for lack of marketability: 32%.
4. Conclusion. The fair market value of one share of Common Stock is $0.07.

The addressable market for mid-market workflow automation is estimated at several billion dollars annually and continues to grow as teams consolidate tooling.

The product roadmap prioritizes integrations with common accounting and CRM platforms, followed by a mobile companion app.
