## Brightline launches Autopilot Scheduling

Portland, OR — 1/17/2023 — Brightline today announced a public beta of its workflow automation product, adding QuickBooks sync.

"Customers asked, we listened." said Ivan Ferreira, VP Marketing.

Join the launch webinar on June 16, 2022. Speakers: Alex Rivera (Engineering), Jordan Blake (Operations), and customer guest Lee Chen of Cobalt Dental.

Go-to-market focuses on inbound content, partner referrals and a small outbound team targeting operations leaders.

Gross margin is expected to improve as hosting costs are renegotiated and support is partially automated.

Competitors include larger horizontal suites and a handful of point solutions; differentiation rests on setup time and price.

### Additional Detail

Onboarding time fell again this period as templates improved. Marketing published a new escalation playbook ahead of schedule. Customer interviews highlighted demand for better mobile workflows. Customer success piloted renewal forecasting for mid-market accounts after customer feedback.
