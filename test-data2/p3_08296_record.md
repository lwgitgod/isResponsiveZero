## FOUNDER STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of September 17, 2021 by and between Brightline Ventures, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Rivera ("Purchaser").

1. Purchase. Purchaser agrees to purchase 5,500,000 shares of the Company's Common Stock at $0.0001 per share, for aggregate cash consideration of $3,484.75, plus the assignment of the technology described in Exhibit A.

2. Vesting. The shares vest in equal monthly installments over 48 months.

3. Representations. Purchaser is acquiring the shares for investment and not with a view to distribution.

4. Further Assurances. Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may reasonably be required to carry out the intent of this instrument.

Exhibit A: Brightline platform source code, designs, domain names and related IP.
